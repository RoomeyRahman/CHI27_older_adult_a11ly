# Research Questions

**RQ1 (Formative).** How do Bangladeshi intergenerational care networks distribute, negotiate, and morally account for medication work before an agent enters the household?

**RQ2 (Interaction).** When an agent joins a Bangladeshi care network, through what everyday practices do older adults and caregivers assign, contest, and change its allegiance, and under what conditions are shifts between independent support and family involvement accepted or resisted?

**RQ3 (Design).** Under what conditions do older adults and caregivers treat the agent's roles as tool, coach, or advocate as legitimate, and what design mechanisms make shifts among these roles visible, revocable, negotiable, and dignity-preserving?
