The Phase 2 older adult master contains 40 codes across 12 subthemes and 5 themes. The caregiver master contains 37 codes across 6 subthemes and 2 themes. The final synthesis connects both role specific analyses through 40 codes, 10 subthemes, and 5 themes. The themes form a sequence from routine support, to negotiated family involvement, to accountable exception handling, to relational progress feedback, and then to care through friends and community. Each theme separates older adult and caregiver perspectives, identifies opportunities for current or future AI tools, and states the design challenge that limits the claim. All code labels omit participant rosters and evidence status tags. Lived and hypothetical evidence remain separated in the reflexivity notes and theme boundaries because several interview probes describe mechanisms that were not deployed.
