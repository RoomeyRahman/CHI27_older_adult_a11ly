# Cross-phase affiliation synthesis: Passes C and D

Twenty-five affiliation codes (X01 to X25) in `03-matrix.md`, each pairing a Phase 1 human form with a Phase 2 agent-directed form and naming the master codes it draws on. Sub-themes group the codes; themes group the sub-themes. Every theme names the phases it draws on and clears the two-source rule across them or carries `[single-instance]`.

## Pass C: sub-themes (11)

### A1. Reminding and first reminder: from presence to the agent, where the family is not
- Constituent codes: X01
- Definition: reminding by whoever was present became the agent's first reminder where kin are absent or busy; the asking that remained was reserved for a miss.
- Phases: 1 and 2. Single-code sub-theme, defended because it is the most widely lived shift.

### A2. Knowledge, custody, and update: kin who set, check, and change the agent
- Constituent codes: X02, X03, X04
- Definition: the division of knowledge by kind persisted with the agent in the least situated seat; custody of the record extended to custody of the agent's knowledge; the child's channel to the doctor stayed human with the agent allowed to report.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### A3. Proportion and direction: told of a miss, one kin, a delay, and consent
- Constituent codes: X05, X06, X07, X25
- Definition: the older adults' Phase 1 proportion, told about a miss and not watched, became the shared vocabulary of both roles; the sequence matched what was built; direction acquired a reason; stock relocated advocacy toward the buyer; consent appeared as a condition with no human antecedent.
- Phases: 1 and 2; X07 `[single-instance]` for the lived misses; X25 an asymmetry.

### G1. Gifting: delegation as prudence becomes granting the agent's move as trust
- Constituent codes: X08, X09
- Definition: delegation accounted for as prudence became the granted request accounted for as trust; checking received as help became kin asking through the agent's channel that is still care, with the notification as the reason to talk.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### C1. Contestation inside care: irritation at asking, at repeats, and at described pushback
- Constituent codes: X10
- Definition: irritation moved from the kin's asking to the agent's asking and to a described pushback, and was resolved by understanding in both phases; the pushback material is believed-capability. Single-code sub-theme kept for its evidence grade.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### C2. Contestation of the care network: the self-manager's refusal
- Constituent codes: X11
- Definition: the claim to competence became a claim to a single principal; where no kin does medication work there is no allegiance to negotiate. Single-code sub-theme kept as the counter-case.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### R1. Revocation: small refusals and unused aids become the decline and the way to say no
- Constituent codes: X12, X13
- Definition: refusal that protected the older adult's standing became a decline that protects the kin's picture, lived through a built mechanism; non-use became an imagined veto grounded in a document.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### R2. The record below the act, and silence as busyness, before and after the agent
- Constituent codes: X14, X15
- Definition: the household's rule that a record does not secure the act and that a question restores it was restated toward the agent's record, and silence toward the agent inherited the vocabulary of the missed dose; people did the reading in both phases.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### E1. Ceremony: visibility of checking and change, and the announcement heard
- Constituent codes: X16, X17, X18
- Definition: the demand that checking and change be visible to the older adult became the announcement heard and the change that must reach everyone with its reason; the taking of a role stayed unannounced in both phases.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### E2. Roles and conditions carried over: tool below the doctor, sound and simplicity, relief of the caregiver's work
- Constituent codes: X19, X20, X21
- Definition: the tool role granted without condition in both phases and the no-guess rule turned on the system; sound and simplicity confirmed by use; the caregiver's knowledge problem became relief of the caregiver's clock without removing the family.
- Phases: 1 and 2.

### E3. Outcomes with weak human antecedents: the streak as family object, memory reinforced, trust by testing
- Constituent codes: X22, X23, X24
- Definition: trust through the person who set the tool became testing against what the household already held; memory claimed as competence became a second memory reinforced rather than replaced; the streak, with only peer talk as antecedent, became a small family object whose affect the household supplied.
- Phases: 1 and 2; X24 rests on Phase 2 with a weak Phase 1 antecedent and says so.

## Pass D: themes (5)

### X1. Allegiance assigned along the household's existing division of reminding, knowing, buying, and deciding
- Organizing concept: the agent's allegiance was assigned by the household's prior division of medication work rather than decided afresh; what was new was a reason for direction, a relocation of advocacy toward supply, and consent named as a condition.
- Constituent sub-themes: A1, A2, A3
- Phases: 1 and 2; two-source rule cleared in both. RQ2 and RQ3 (direction).

### X2. Checking through the agent received as care, contested as irritation, and refused by the self-manager
- Organizing concept: the agent's channel inherited the household's reception of checking, care and irritation in one sentence, and was refused only where no care network did the work.
- Constituent sub-themes: G1, C1, C2
- Phases: 1 and 2. RQ2 (gifting, contestation).

### X3. Revocation and the record: small refusals, the decline, and the act held above what is recorded
- Organizing concept: the household's rule that a record does not secure the act governed the agent's record too, and revocation became a built decline whose ground shifted from the older adult's standing to the kin's worry.
- Constituent sub-themes: R1, R2
- Phases: 1 and 2. RQ2 (revocation) and RQ3 (revocability).

### X4. Ceremony as legibility: the announcement, the visible change, and the caregiver's role that just happened
- Organizing concept: ceremony toward the agent is speech for the announcement and maintenance for change, while the taking of a role stayed unannounced in both phases.
- Constituent sub-themes: E1
- Phases: 1 and 2. RQ3 (visibility, ceremony).

### X5. Roles, conditions, and outcomes carried across the move: the tool below the doctor, sound, relief, trust, memory, and the streak
- Organizing concept: the conditions Phase 1 set on any agent were confirmed by use, and the outcomes Phase 2 adds are relational and small: relief of the caregiver's clock, trust by testing, memory reinforced, and a streak made a family object.
- Constituent sub-themes: E2, E3
- Phases: 1 and 2; X24 with a weak Phase 1 antecedent. RQ3 (roles and outcomes).
