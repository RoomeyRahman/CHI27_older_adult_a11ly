# Cross-phase affiliation synthesis: the five practices among humans and toward the agent

Reads `phase1/master/03-matrix.md`, `phase1/master/02-themes.md`, `phase2/master/03-matrix.md`, and `phase2/master/02-themes.md`. Transcripts were not re-read except to check an extract. The two phases are not pooled: Phase 1 is the human affiliation baseline (RQ1) and Phase 2 the agent-directed half (RQ2, RQ3), and the comparison is the analysis. Master code ids are Phase 1 `M01` to `M40` and Phase 2 `M01` to `M40`; the phase is always stated. Household pairing is unfiled: where a caregiver's account and an older adult's account describe the same kind of episode, that is said without saying they are one household.

## Practices with no agent-directed instance

None of the five is absent toward the agent. Two are thin: revocation is lived only as the decline (M06) and otherwise imagined; contestation of the agent's information is lived once (M32). Two have no clean human antecedent in the corpus and are recorded as asymmetries rather than matches: consent as a condition (P2 M11) and the streak as a family object (P2 M25 to M29), whose nearest Phase 1 antecedents are peer talk about medicine and a peer's dose as an indirect reminder (P1 M15, M17).

## Roles and dimensions, read off the symmetry

The **tool** role is legitimate in both phases without condition (P1 M38; P2 M22). The **coach** role, if the streak and encouragement are its instrument, is accepted as small encouragement from kin and refused as pressure, competition, or another thing to check (P2 M25 to M28); its affect was supplied by the household, not the mechanic. The **advocate** role is accepted under three triggers, a real miss, a long silence, and finishing stock, after the older adult's first chance, toward one kin, and, in three accounts, only with the older adult's agreement (P2 M08 to M11, M40); it is refused by the self-managers (M18). Of the four dimensions, **direction** is set by the household's division and acquired a reason; **visibility** was lived as the announcement and demanded for change and for warnings; **revocability** was lived as the decline and imagined as a document-grounded veto; **ceremony** is speech for the announcement, maintenance for change, and silence for the taking of a role.
