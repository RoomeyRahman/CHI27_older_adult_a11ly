# P10, Phase 2: familiarization memo

Source: `Supplementary/Interviews/phase-2/Participants/P10.md`. Older adult, 78, man; the same participant as Phase 1 P10 (diabetes and breathing medicine, son and daughter-in-law co-resident). Own smartphone during deployment. Translated from Bangla, edited into full paragraphs under the guide's section titles. No consent-exclusion record is filed.

**Evidence-grade warning for this unit.** This is the first Phase 2 account in which the participant narrates as lived the mechanisms the build record does not include. He says he 'stopped the family alert' several times, and that the agent 'pushed back on my veto because the health risk was becoming higher' and 'became more serious' when he kept ignoring it. The request-and-grant prompt and the decline were built and may carry lived claims. The risk-graded weakening veto was not built (CLAUDE.md 3.3). The interviewer's probe presupposed it ('when the agent pushed back on your veto'). I therefore code the decline as `[lived]` and the pushback as `[believed-capability]`: what he believed the agent did, not what it did. The same rule applies to 'It did not immediately treat it like I had done something wrong', which describes repeat reminders that were built, so `[lived]`, and to his sense that 'it understood' his silence, which attributes interpretation the build did not do, so `[believed-capability]`.

## What this account is about

The richest lived account so far. The announcement was strange and then clarifying: 'When it said it is working for me, I understood it better... And when it said it is also working with my family, I understood that also.' In front of everyone it can make him feel 'exposed. Not very bad.' The shift episode is narrated in full: he had not taken the diabetes tablet, was watching the news, 'The agent reminded me first. I did not respond because I thought I would take it after finishing the news. Then later it asked for permission to involve the family, something like that. I agreed. After that my son came and asked me if I had taken it.' Granting was trust, not lost independence: 'My son is not putting the tablet in my mouth. He is just checking.' He declined 'a few times' when he had taken it and not responded, 'because there was no need to worry them.'

Checking: 'They ask less... But they still ask sometimes, especially if the agent tells them something.' Asking in a normal way is family: 'If my son comes and says, baba, did you take it, that is different from a phone message. It feels more caring.' Care changed, not reduced: 'It has reduced the small checking, but it has not reduced the care... The agent is doing one small part.' A prescription change: the agent reminded of the old timing, the son checked with the doctor; 'it helped us remember what we were doing before, but it did not decide for us.' The streak: 'baba is doing better than all of us'; 'very good, don't break it now'; when it broke 'I was a little upset because I had kept it for many days. But my son said, okay, it happened... If I was alone, maybe I would have felt more annoyed. With family, they made it into a small thing.' He would not want the score 'to become another thing they keep checking all the time'. Probation: he watched, compared with his routine, the son checked settings. Memory: 'both a little'; he waits for the reminder sometimes and also knows it is almost time before it rings. Closing: 'Give me the first chance, then give me another reminder. If I still do not respond, then tell my son or whoever is responsible.'

## Analytic noticings, as questions

1. 'Then later it asked for permission to involve the family... I agreed.' Is this the request-and-grant prompt lived, and is the grant an act toward his son rather than toward the agent?
2. 'there was no need to worry them.' Is the decline used to protect the family from worry rather than to protect his privacy?
3. 'With family, they made it into a small thing.' Is streak grief absorbed by the family rather than by the individual, so that the mechanic's affect depends on the household?
4. 'the agent is doing one small part.' Is the agent's allegiance bounded by the size of its part in care?
5. 'tell my son or whoever is responsible.' Does he name direction by role at the end, having named it by person throughout?
