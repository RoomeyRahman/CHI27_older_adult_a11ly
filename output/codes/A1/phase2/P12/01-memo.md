## What this account is about

A woman who already used a mobile alarm describes what the agent added: an announcement she liked knowing, a request to involve family that she allowed on a busy night, and her husband's question that made her remember. She declined when she had taken it 'Otherwise my family may think I forgot.' Described pushback annoyed and then made sense: 'If it just keeps quiet, then I may forget completely.' Silence is being busy: 'Sometimes I hear it but say later.' Checking: 'now the agent asks first, so maybe they don't have to ask every time'; her husband's asking 'is also care'; the notification 'can start the conversation. But it should not make him worry for every small thing.' Prescription: the agent tells what is already there; she asks doctor or pharmacist. Streak: her husband said 'good, you are taking it properly'; it broke when busy; 'It did not become a big problem'; she does not want them 'checking the score all the time.' Probation: compared with her alarm and routine. Memory: 'Sometimes I know the time is coming before it rings.' Voice over notifications: 'Sometimes there are many notifications and I don't look at the phone. If someone says take your medicine, I hear it.' Closing: 'Not too many reminders, not too quick to tell family. But also don't stay quiet if I really forget.'

## Analytic noticings, as questions

3. 'If it just keeps quiet, then I may forget completely.' Does she accept described pushback because silence is the failure she fears?
