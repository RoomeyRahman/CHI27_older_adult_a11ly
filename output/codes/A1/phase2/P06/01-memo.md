# P06, Phase 2: familiarization memo

Source: `Supplementary/Interviews/phase-2/Participants/P06.md`. Older adult, 67, man, farmer; the same participant as Phase 1 P06 (wife on the same medicine, son the reader). Household smartphone shared or proxy-operated during deployment; in Phase 1 he had no phone and used his son's. Translated from Bangla, lightly cleaned. No consent-exclusion record is filed. Evidence-grade rule as in the Phase 2 P01 memo.

## What this account is about

The farmer's account after two weeks confirms his Phase 1 placement of the agent: 'At home my wife already reminds me, and outside the phone can remind me'; 'In the field there is nobody asking.' The son set it up and became the one who checked: 'When my son started checking it. If I did not confirm, he would ask me. Sometimes I had taken the tablet but did not press anything... So it became another way for him to check.' The three kinds of knowledge he gave in Phase 1 are restated with the agent in the third seat: 'The phone can tell time. My son knows the medicine name. My wife knows when I ate and when I came home. So all three, you can say, have different information.' He trusts what the son puts in; 'If the phone itself starts deciding things, no.'

His silence in the field is physical: he may not hear; he takes and forgets the button. The couple still remind each other, and the 'yes yes' pattern is now his own report of receiving his wife's asking: 'She asks, medicine taken, medicine taken. I say yes yes. But she is helping. I also ask her about her medicine. So it is both ways.' The son's checking became 'easier to check without asking every time... less unnecessary asking.' The streak was 'a little interesting'; it made him aware 'on days when work changes'. Memory: 'It is more like a second memory.' The farmer group would raise his trust in the app as in a seed. His closing: 'the phone should remind, not decide.'

## Analytic noticings, as questions

1. 'another way for him to check.' Does the agent's notification give the son a new channel for a check he already made, and is that the whole of the allegiance shift here?
2. 'less unnecessary asking.' Does the agent reduce the asking that is unnecessary while leaving the asking that is care, and who decides which is which?
3. 'all three have different information.' Is the agent one member of a knowledge division rather than the household's memory?
4. 'I say yes yes. But she is helping.' Is the 'yes yes' now narrated with its reception, and does the reciprocity ('I also ask her') change what it means?
5. 'a second memory.' Is this the restored-not-replaced reading in his words?

## Absences

Whether the son saw a heat map or received a notification on his own phone; what the announcement said; any use of the decline; the wife's view.
