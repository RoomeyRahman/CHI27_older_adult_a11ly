# P04, Phase 2: familiarization memo

Source: `Supplementary/Interviews/phase-2/Participants/P04.md`. Filed as P04; header 'Not stated, 69, Male', consistent with Phase 1 P04 (wife co-resident, son nearby). Own smartphone during deployment. Translated from Bangla, lightly cleaned. No consent-exclusion record is filed. Variant guide, mostly conditional; evidence-grade rule as in the Phase 2 P01 memo.

## What this account is about

The deployment entered a household in which the wife reminds and the son holds the prescription, and it took the first turn: 'Before my wife would ask me, did you take the morning one. Now the reminder comes and I check. If I don't respond, then family can know.' The wife 'does not have to remember every time' and 'still does sometimes'. He states the limit of the phone against the wife precisely: 'She knows my daily routine. Phone does not know if I have eaten or if I am outside. So her reminder is different.' Trust came through the son: 'My son checked it first. I also watched what it was saying... slowly you trust it.' Direction: after one or two reminders, the son; 'Wife may also know because she is at home, but no need to alert everybody.' Bounds: the doctor decides; 'If it changes something without telling us, also bad'; no recommendations. The streak is 'okay', family may see it, 'don't make it like a competition', 'Our medicine is our business.' His summary: 'Now the phone does the first reminder. Family still checks when needed. So it helps without taking over.'

## Analytic noticings, as questions

1. 'Phone does not know if I have eaten or if I am outside.' Is the wife's advantage contextual knowledge, and does that fix the agent's place as time-keeper only?
2. 'If it changes something without telling us, also bad.' Is visibility of the agent's own changes a condition he adds unprompted?
3. 'Son is enough. Wife may also know because she is at home.' Is direction set by knowledge (son) and presence (wife) as in Phase 1?
4. 'helps without taking over.' Is this the acceptable allegiance in one phrase?
