## What this account is about

The deployment entered this household through the daughter, as everything did in Phase 1. The assistant 'was just something on the phone. Then it became part of the routine because my daughter was also checking it.' The daughter set it up, compared it with the prescription, and should be the one to update it: 'I don't want to put the wrong thing.' What changed is who reminds first: 'they don't have to remember every time now. The phone reminds me first. If I don't take it, then they can check'; 'My daughter does not have to remember every time.' Her husband, also on medicine, and she still remind each other, and 'They ask because they care.'

The record-below-the-act condition she stated in Phase 1 is restated with the assistant in view: 'Sometimes I take it and forget to press... My daughter can ask me. The app should not immediately say I did not take it.' Deciding stays the doctor's: if the assistant and the daughter disagree, 'I listen to my daughter after she checks with the doctor. The doctor decides the medicine.' The score is 'okay'; the daughter or husband may see it; a broken streak would make her 'check why', not worry about the number. Her memory: 'I still know some medicines by the strip and colour', and 'Before I remembered mostly from tea, food, dinner. Now the reminder also tells me.' She calls herself 'A little' more independent: 'I still take the medicine myself. I need them for checking and arranging, but I don't need someone standing there every time.'

## Analytic noticings, as questions

1. 'part of the routine because my daughter was also checking it.' Did the assistant become the household's rather than hers at the moment the daughter took it into her checking?
2. 'I don't need someone standing there every time.' Is the phone's first reminder what she means by independence, and is that a change in who is present rather than in what she does?
3. 'I listen to my daughter after she checks with the doctor.' Is the assistant's word ranked below the daughter's, and the daughter's below the doctor's, in one sentence?
4. Her wants repeat her Phase 1 list nearly item for item. Does two weeks of use change what she asks for, or only add the daughter's checking to it?
