# Phase 2 master synthesis: reflexivity

## Methodological commitments, held everywhere

Reflexive thematic analysis, not codebook or reliability TA; codes evolved and the development logs record how. One analyst; no inter-rater reliability, kappa, or second coder is proposed, and the alternatives offered are this audit trail, the unit memos, the reflexivity notes in every matrix, and a critical-friend reading by the second analysing agent in its own slot. Themes are generated, not found; each has a central organizing concept stated in `02-themes.md`. Prevalence is not importance: the participant counts in `03-matrix.md` are reported, and weight is argued from mechanism and grade, but the two-source rule was applied to every master code and the two exceptions are tagged. Latent over semantic where the data allowed. No saturation is claimed; sample adequacy is argued in `04-review.md`. Inductive first, the affiliation codebook second, and the practice column left empty where nothing fit.

## Study context, as filed

Phase 2 is the post-deployment half of a two-phase qualitative household study in Bangladesh, run between January and June 2026 with a two-week prototype deployment between the phases (CLAUDE.md Section 3.1). The filed corpus holds 17 older-adult transcripts (P01 to P17) and 8 caregiver transcripts (C01 to C08) for Phase 2; `/output/Method.md` says seven caregivers took part in both phases and one only in Phase 1, which the corpus contradicts, and I surface that rather than resolve it. The deployed build, per CLAUDE.md 3.3, included manual setup, scheduled reminders with name and time, a logbook, a streak, a heat map, family notification of an unconfirmed dose, a spoken Bangla announcement of whom the agent served, and a request-and-grant prompt the older adult could decline. The risk-graded weakening veto, patterned-silence interpretation, probationary mode, and shared family scores were not built. Several Phase 2 probes are phrased as though participants had experienced those mechanisms; I coded every such answer as believed-capability, elicited, or hypothetical, and `M07` carries the tag in its name. No decision logs exist; no finding rests on one. Household pairing is unfiled; no paired episode is asserted, and where a caregiver's account and an older adult's account describe the same kind of episode I say so without saying they are the same household.

## Positionality

I read translated, edited transcripts; the edits are heavier in Phase 2 than in Phase 1, and several files follow the guide's section titles with one clean paragraph per turn (P09 to P13, C01, C02, C07, C08). I treated them as faithful summaries of positions and did not read interactional detail into them. I read as a researcher who expects families to negotiate and who was primed by the project's framing to find assignment, contestation, gifting, revocation, and ceremony; the practice column was left empty in 22 of 40 master codes so that the symmetry with Phase 1 is not manufactured. I read the caregivers as adult children and grandchildren with jobs, phones, and a stake in relief, and I checked each time a caregiver claimed care whether the older adults' accounts supported the claim; they do, in the same terms, though never as a filed pair.

## Assumptions I made and could not test

That the announcement was heard as the build record says it was spoken; participants describe it as telling them who it worked for, which matches, but no log confirms delivery. That the decline was presented as the build record says; six older adults describe stopping the alert, which matches. That no pushback occurred; the build record says none was built, and I have trusted it over eight participants who describe one, because the probe put the mechanism in their mouths.

## Where the data resisted

The data resisted a story of surveillance. Not one older adult described the notification as being watched, and the two who refused family involvement did so as self-managers, not as people watched. The data resisted a story of gamification: the streak's pull was admitted by a few and subordinated by all, and the caregivers were as often the score's subject or audience as the older adults. The data resisted a clean older-adult and caregiver division in two households (C05, C06), where the caregiver administers and the agent's first principal is the caregiver; the older adults of those households are not in the corpus as speakers and their acceptance is reported at second hand. The data resisted the four-dimension vocabulary in one respect: participants named consent (`M11`) and explanation (`M24`) as conditions, which the dimensions accommodate under visibility and ceremony but do not name.

## What I would tell the second analyst without showing them my themes

Read P10, P11, C03, and C04 first for the lived episodes; grade every Phase 2 answer before coding it; and count how many of your codes about the agent's decisions are actually about a person's decision the agent reported.
