# Phase 2 master synthesis: Passes C and D (sub-themes, themes, development log)

Built over the 40 master codes in `01-code-synthesis.md`. Every sub-theme lists its constituent master codes by id; every theme lists its sub-themes. Phase 2 answers RQ2 and RQ3. Evidence grade is carried into every definition: lived material is what participants did or saw during the two-week deployment; elicited material is a position taken under an offered frame; hypothetical material answers a conditional probe; believed-capability material attributes to the agent a behaviour the build record excludes.

## Pass C: sub-themes (12)

### S1. The announcement heard: clearer, a little exposing, and a condition on telling
- Constituent master codes: M01, M02
- Definition: the spoken statement of whom the agent serves was strange and then clarifying, and made the family's behaviour legible; in front of others it exposes a little; the older adult should know before the family is told.
- Excludes: M03, the shift itself.
- RQ3 (visibility).

### S2. Granted as trust, another way of a help already given; declined to spare worry
- Constituent master codes: M03, M04, M05, M06
- Definition: six older adults narrate the request-and-grant prompt as lived; granting was trust and another way of a help the family already gave; the agent became shared when kin checked through it; the decline was used when the record was wrong, to spare kin worry.
- Excludes: M07, the believed pushback.
- RQ2 (assignment, gifting, revocation).

### S3. Direction and proportion: first chance, one kin, a real miss, consent
- Constituent master codes: M08, M09, M10, M11, M36, M37
- Definition: the acceptable agent gives the older adult the first chance and a repeat, then tells one kin chosen by knowledge, presence, or role, only of a real miss, a long silence, or finishing stock, and, in three accounts, only when the older adult agrees; in households where a caregiver administers, the agent's first principal is the caregiver; everyone answered both, with the older adult first.
- Excludes: M12 to M14, which concern what silence means.
- RQ2.

### S4. The phone reminds first; kin ask less and still ask; the notification starts a conversation
- Constituent master codes: M15, M17
- Definition: the first reminder relocated from kin to the agent, the routine asking fell away, kin still ask and ask again when the agent tells them something, and the notification is valued as an occasion for a question rather than as a report.
- Excludes: M16, the reception of asking.
- RQ2.

### S5. Asking as care that irritates; the self-manager who refuses a family project
- Constituent master codes: M16, M18
- Definition: kin asking received as care and as irritation in one sentence, and as a different act from a phone message; the agent does one small part and cannot carry the worry behind asking; against this, two or three self-managing men accept the agent as a layer and refuse a family project.
- Excludes: nothing.
- RQ2 (checking as care and as oversight, decided case by case).

### S6. Silence as busyness and the record below the act; a false alarm is a failure
- Constituent master codes: M07, M12, M13, M14
- Definition: non-response is work, deferral, not hearing, or a forgotten press; a missed press is not a missed dose and a person checks before the record is believed; a false report to kin is the failure participants fear; the believed pushback sits here as reasoning about a described escalation.
- Excludes: nothing.
- RQ2 and RQ3.

### S7. The phone does not know if I have eaten: kin's situated knowledge
- Constituent master codes: M19
- Definition: the spouse or child knows whether the older adult has eaten or is outside; the phone knows only what is put into it; this fixes the agent as time-keeper. Single-code sub-theme, defended because it is the argument every other bound rests on.
- RQ2 and RQ3.

### S8. The doctor decides; a way to say no; mismatches checked
- Constituent master codes: M22, M23, M32
- Definition: authority stays with the doctor, the written prescription, or the household's notes; the agent reminds what was decided and never guesses; a wrong reminder is stopped and checked against the document; a caregiver found mismatches and checked them.
- Excludes: nothing.
- RQ3 (the tool role, revocability).

### S9. Knowledge only as current as its updater: the ceremony of change and the warning that explains
- Constituent master codes: M20, M21, M24
- Definition: the agent reminded of an old schedule until someone updated it; a change must reach the household as well as the agent, with what changed and why, or kin remind of and buy the old; a warning must say what the problem is.
- Excludes: nothing.
- RQ3 (visibility and ceremony as maintenance and explanation).

### S10. The streak noticed, praised, broken and made small; not a game; family may see
- Constituent master codes: M25, M26, M27, M28, M29
- Definition: the streak became a small thing kin noticed and praised; a break stung and was made small by family; more participants read a changed count as something to check than as a loss; sharing is for encouragement, not pressure; the score is the family's to see and personal beyond it.
- Excludes: nothing.
- RQ3.

### S11. Trust by testing against routine, alarm, notes, or prescription, and through the kin who set it
- Constituent master codes: M30, M31
- Definition: participants watched the reminders for days against what they already knew or a document, some staging the test from home to outside, some keeping paper nearby; trust also ran through the kin who set it and checked its entries.
- Excludes: nothing.
- RQ3.

### S12. A second memory; the caregiver relieved of the clock; less pressure, not less responsibility; the agent's register and its limits
- Constituent master codes: M33, M34, M35, M38, M39, M40
- Definition: for older adults the agent is a second memory that reinforced and anticipated rather than replaced; for caregivers it removed clock-watching, calling, and the intrusive thought; both roles sum it as less pressure with the family still involved; its register is sound, its limits are wrong information, noise, and maintenance, and its useful advocacy includes stock.
- Excludes: nothing.
- RQ3.

## Pass D: themes (4)

### T1. The agent enters as one more way of a checking the family already did, and becomes shared when kin check through it
- Organizing concept: the agent's allegiance is derivative. Participants accepted the announcement, granted the request, and shared the agent with kin because the family already reminded, asked, and knew; the shift to shared use happened through kin's checking, and its proportion, direction, and consent conditions restate the household's existing arrangement.
- Constituent sub-themes: S1, S2, S3
- Definition and boundary: "it made sense because the medicines are not only my responsibility. We all check for each other" (C03, Phase 2) and "it asked for permission to involve the family, something like that. I agreed. After that my son came and asked me" (P10, Phase 2). Boundary: no filed pairing; the caregiver of a given older adult is not identified.
- Answers RQ2 (assignment, gifting, revocation) and RQ3 (visibility).

### T2. The first reminder moves to the agent while asking remains care, and the record yields to the person
- Organizing concept: what changed is who reminds first; what did not change is that kin ask, that asking is care as well as irritation, and that silence and the record are read by people. Where no kin did medication work, the agent stays a private layer.
- Constituent sub-themes: S4, S5, S6
- Definition and boundary: "It has reduced the small checking, but it has not reduced the care" (P10, Phase 2), "The app doesn't replace that feeling" (P05, Phase 2), "the phone does not know everything" (P05, Phase 2), and "I don't want my medicine routine to become a family project" (P11, Phase 2). Boundary: the believed pushback is reasoning, not use.
- Answers RQ2 (contestation inside care; silence as participation).

### T3. Authority stays with doctor, document, and kin: the agent reminds what was decided, knows only what was entered, and must make change visible
- Organizing concept: the agent's legitimate role is the tool. It is placed below kin's situated knowledge and the doctor's or the document's authority, its knowledge is only as current as its human updater, and a change or a warning must be made visible to the household with its reason.
- Constituent sub-themes: S7, S8, S9
- Definition and boundary: "Phone does not know if I have eaten or if I am outside" (P04, Phase 2), "The prescription is the authority for me" (P15, Phase 2), "until the change was updated" (P11, Phase 2), and "it should explain what the problem is" (C01, Phase 2). Boundary: hypothetical in the main; two lived stale-schedule episodes.
- Answers RQ3 (roles, visibility, revocability, ceremony).

### T4. The streak as a small family object, trust by testing and through kin, memory reinforced, and the caregiver relieved of the clock
- Organizing concept: the agent's outcomes are relational and small: a streak the family talks about and makes small when broken, trust earned against what the household already knew, memory reinforced rather than replaced, and a caregiver's invisible timekeeping removed without removing the family.
- Constituent sub-themes: S10, S11, S12
- Definition and boundary: "With family, they made it into a small thing" (P10, Phase 2), "Then I tested it when I was outside" (P11, Phase 2), "It is more like a second memory" (P06, Phase 2), and "I don't keep watching the clock for their medicine" (C03, Phase 2). Boundary: shared scores were not built; the streak and heat map were.
- Answers RQ3 (design mechanisms and outcomes).
