## What this account is about

A son who helps his mother 'because she sometimes forgets', with a method that is no method: the instruction on the back of the leaf, habit, a fixed place. She forgets 'Quite often', in kitchen work, talk, or with a guest, 'and then the time goes... nobody notices. Later she says oh I forgot.' When she remembers late she asks him whether to take or wait, and they check what the doctor said or ask the pharmacist. He knows the names; she knows the packet, the tablet, and the place, and asks for 'the white one or the strip that I take at night'.

What this account adds is the failure of a plain alarm, stated precisely: 'I can set one but then maybe she is doing something and ignores it. Or sometimes the alarm is there but she doesn't know which medicine it is for.' A reminder that names the medicine, in his mother's hearing, 'mother take your night medicine', would be clearer, and at gatherings 'A voice reminder would cut through that.' He wants to be told after a repeat, 'Otherwise I keep thinking did she take it or not. The app can reduce that pressure', and a simple yes or no history. He would set it up and manage the settings; she would hear and press one button. He admits the group influences him 'More than I should maybe', but for his mother he would check because 'her medicines are already fixed'. His closing: 'family is already doing the reminder work. The app should help us with that. It should make it less stressful.'

## Analytic noticings, as questions

1. 'She doesn't know which medicine it is for.' Does a sound without content fail because it addresses no one and names nothing, and is that why a voice that says 'mother' would work?
2. 'Nobody notices.' Is the household's attention, not the mother's memory, what fails at the dose?
3. 'Did she take it or not.' Is the son's problem, as for C01, knowledge rather than reminding?
4. 'Her medicines are already fixed.' Is the mother's regimen protected from social advice in a way his own is not, and why?
5. 'It creates tension.' The medicine left at home: whose tension, and between whom?
6. 'Family is already doing the reminder work.' Is the agent asked to lighten a labour, and what does 'less stressful' name?
