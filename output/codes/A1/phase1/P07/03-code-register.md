# P07, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 70. After reconciliation (38 merges): 34 including two merge-target names. A second pass merged four more pairs sharing a mechanism, giving 29. Retained: 29. Parked: 5.

Parking fell on inventory (household shape, which the memo carries), on modality instrument codes, and on a closing acceptance line whose content is carried by the retained closing code. Frequency was not used; the peer block, the longest in the transcript, became one code.

Hardest calls. (1) `'Before I remembered everything' [tr]` is a self-report of decline and sits uneasily against the framing commitment that this is a design gap, not a memory problem. Retained: the commitment governs how the analysis frames older adults, not whether a participant's own account of herself may be recorded, and parking it would smooth away a disconfirming case. (2) `'He keeps telling me don't forget' [tr]` merged into `Son asks, daughter calls 'Ma'` rather than kept as an admonition code; the son's admonishing is part of how he reminds, and the reception (no complaint) is in the definition. (3) `Prefers strips to the box` merged into `Son separates and writes labels`: her small refusal of the box is a refusal inside the son's arrangement, and it is more legible there than alone.
