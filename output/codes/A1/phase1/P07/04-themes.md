# P07, Phase 1: Passes C, D, and E

## Pass C: sub-themes

Over the 30 retained codes only.

### ST1. The son as successor holding names, papers, and changes
- Constituent codes: `Son took over after husband's death`, `Son knows count and names`, `Son took the papers after a loss`, `Son separates and writes labels`, `Pharmacy knows our medicine`, `'I depend on him for many things' [tr]`
- Definition: since the husband's death the son holds the names, the prescription (taken into his custody after she lost one), the repair of changes by separation and written labels, and the dealings with a pharmacy that knows the household; she accepts the word dependence and itemizes his holdings.
- Excludes: `Son asks, daughter calls 'Ma'`, which is reminding rather than holding.
- RQ1.

### ST2. Reminding divided between the co-resident son and a daughter who calls and prepares
- Constituent codes: `Son asks, daughter calls 'Ma'`, `Son sets out, she checks with them`, `Daughter arranges, is backup`, `Daughter says bag; 'yes yes'`
- Definition: the son sets out the doses and asks; the daughter calls almost daily, worries more when a medicine is new, arranges the tablets when she visits, prepares them when the participant stays with her, and repeats the instruction to carry medicine, met with an affirmative that does not secure the act.
- Excludes: `'I don't have to think' [tr]`, her evaluation of the daughter's preparing, placed with reception.
- RQ1.

### ST3. Recognition and asking rather than guessing as her own part
- Constituent codes: `Knows strip, cannot read names`, `'Why take chance' [tr]`, `Timing may slip, never the tablet`, `Asks son, never two`, `Manages regular alone, waits on new`, `'medicine I ask family first' [tr]`, `Peer advice routed to son`
- Definition: she recognizes her strip and cannot read names, asks the son even with glasses because he knows, bounds her possible error to timing, never doubles, could manage the familiar regimen alone but waits on anything new, and routes every outside suggestion through the family to the doctor.
- Excludes: `Son took the papers after a loss`, the son's act rather than hers.
- RQ1.

### ST4. Lapses in company and a self-report of decline
- Constituent codes: `Forgetting in cooking or neighbour talk`, `'Before I remembered everything' [tr]`
- Definition: doses are missed in cooking, neighbour talk, and gatherings, discovered by the tablet still in its place and restored by a family question; and she reports that she remembered everything before and now forgets small things.
- Excludes: nothing retained; two-code sub-theme defended because the second code is a disconfirming case that must not be absorbed into a disruption-only reading.
- RQ1.

### ST5. Delegation and checking received as safety and relief
- Constituent codes: `'I feel safe when he checks' [tr]`, `'somebody should keep an eye' [tr]`, `'I don't have to think' [tr]`, `'you people decide and tell me' [tr]`
- Definition: the son's checking is safety, an eye on her is wanted, the daughter's preparing means she does not have to think, and a disagreement between the children is theirs to settle; each is received as relief rather than as loss.
- Excludes: `'I depend on him for many things' [tr]`, which itemizes the arrangement rather than evaluating it.
- RQ1; bears on RQ2 through the grounds on which oversight is welcomed.

### ST6. Conditions on an agent: son installs, agent first then children, she still asks the son
- Constituent codes: `Children's demos forgotten`, `Trust if son set it`, `Phone first, then child calls`, `Missed press must not mislead son`, `'I don't trust myself with new things' [tr]`, `Children control, she looks and takes`
- Definition: past demos were forgotten; an agent would be trusted because a child set it, would remind first and let a child call second, must not let a missed press mislead the son, would still be checked with the son because she does not trust herself with new things, and would be handed to the son if it demanded too much. One reported code, the rest hypothetical.
- Excludes: nothing retained.
- RQ3, with RQ2 bearing through ordering and control.

Sub-theme count: 6.

## Pass D: themes

### T1. The son inheriting the husband's place as holder of the regimen, with the daughter reminding and preparing from nearby
- Organizing concept: medication work in this household is an office that passed from husband to son, and the daughter occupies a second position, by phone and by visit, that intensifies with change.
- Constituent sub-themes: `The son as successor holding names, papers, and changes`, `Reminding divided between the co-resident son and a daughter who calls and prepares`
- Definition and boundary: 'after my husband died he looks after these things' is the theme's ground. The boundary is the daughter-in-law and grandchild, present in the house and absent from the work; and the fact that the son's taking of the papers is narrated as his decision, not negotiated.
- Extracts: L239, L31, L47, L283.

### T2. Her own part kept to recognition and taking, with asking preferred to guessing
- Organizing concept: what remains hers is recognizing the strip and swallowing the tablet, and she protects that part by a rule of asking rather than guessing that she applies to names, to changes, and to outside advice alike.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Recognition and asking rather than guessing as her own part`, `Lapses in company and a self-report of decline`
- Definition and boundary: 'Why take chance' and 'I ask first' are the rule. The boundary is her own account of decline, which I have kept inside this theme as a limit on it rather than smoothing it into disruption.
- Extracts: L135, L127, L63, L279.

### T3. Delegation and checking received as safety and relief rather than as loss
- Organizing concept: the participant welcomes being checked, being known, and not having to decide or think, and grounds each in kinship and in the fear of being alone when something happens.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Delegation and checking received as safety and relief`
- Definition and boundary: single sub-theme theme, kept apart because it is the account's answer to care-or-oversight and the answer is the most affirmative in the phase. Boundary: the frame 'watching' was the interviewer's; her rejection is on record with its grounds, and her position as a widow in her son's house is a condition the other accounts do not share.
- Extracts: L175, L295, L287, L111.

### T4. An agent the son installs that precedes rather than replaces the children's call
- Organizing concept: the agent enters through a child's hands, reminds first so that the children's time is spared, and is subordinate to the son's word because she does not trust herself with new things.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Conditions on an agent: son installs, agent first then children, she still asks the son`
- Definition and boundary: single sub-theme theme for evidence grade. 'Phone reminder can do first. If I don't take then child can call' is the centre. Boundary: everything is hypothetical except the forgotten demos.
- Extracts: L251, L199, L195, L267.

Theme count: 4.
