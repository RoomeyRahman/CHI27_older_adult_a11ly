# P08, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 82, the highest in the phase, because the interview is long, the questions short, and nearly every turn adds a small item. After reconciliation (48 merges): 34, including three merge-target names. A second pass merged one summary code into the partition it summarizes and the purpose code into the packet code, and parked three thin context codes, giving 30. Retained: 30. Parked: 3.

Parking fell on opening context (age, body), on the closing hospitality, and on the phone inventory. Frequency was not used; the partition between taking and getting is stated seven times and became one code, retained for its mechanism and not its count.

Hardest calls. (1) `Age on son's authority`: a small latent index of where knowledge about her sits, but a single line at the opening with no mechanism beyond what `Son knows what doctor gave` carries. Parked, named in Pass E. (2) `'you should have told before' [tr]` against `'We forget to buy' [tr]`: both concern stock failure; kept separate, because one is the son's reproach and its attribution of the failure to her, and the other her plural narration of the same failure, and the difference between them is the finding. (3) `'I said this is not mine' [tr]` could sit under recognition or under deference; retained as its own code because it is the one act of refusal in an account otherwise built on taking what is brought.
