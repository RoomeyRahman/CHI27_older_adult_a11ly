## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 62. After reconciliation (32 merges): 31 originals plus one merge-target name. Retained: 29. Parked: 2.

Retention ran high because this account names more distinct mechanisms than the previous three: a three-way division of knowledge, indirect peer reminding, experiential trust, a two-sided escalation rule, and trust transferred through configuration. Parking was on inventory. Frequency was not used; the group-opinion block recurs heavily because the interviewer probed it, and I merged it down to three codes rather than letting the instrument's salience carry.

Hardest calls. (1) `'not watching me all the time' [tr]` and `Tell son after many misses` come from one turn; kept separate because a refusal and a rule are different evidence and the master synthesis needs both. (2) Whether to retain `Wife asks, he says yes wrongly` separately from `Unsure whether already taken`; kept separate, because the first is the household's check on his report and the second is his own doubt, and the relation between them is the finding. (3) `Group use would sway adoption` and `'Same like medicine' [tr]` merged, with the in-vivo name kept because it states the mechanism (trust travels as it does for medicine) rather than the fact.
