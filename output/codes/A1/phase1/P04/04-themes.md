## Pass C: sub-themes

### ST1. Knowledge of the regimen divided by kind across son, wife, and self
- Constituent codes: `Knowledge divided son, wife, self`, `Son keeps names and prescription`, `Son remembers the consultation`, `Recognizes strip by shape and colour`, `'Why to remember all' [tr]`, `New medicine read by son or pharmacy`
- Definition: the son holds names, the current prescription, and the memory of consultations that change the regimen; the wife holds the daily slots; the participant holds the strip by shape and colour, refuses the need to know names, and hands a new strip to the son or the pharmacy to read.
- Excludes: `Wife asks, he says yes wrongly`, which is checking rather than knowledge.
- RQ1.

### ST2. The peer group as a source of experiential trust and indirect reminders
- Constituent codes: `Peer group all on medicine`, `Consensus from lived use`, `Brings friends' medicines to doctor`, `Reminding without being told`
- Definition: a morning group in which everyone takes medicine supplies testimonials that build trust by number and lived use, candidates the participant brings to the doctor through the son, and reminders that arrive when someone else mentions their own tablet.
- Excludes: `'Same like medicine' [tr]`, which applies the group's trust mechanism to a tool and is hypothetical.
- RQ1.

### ST3. The network summarized as complementary uses
- Constituent codes: `'Everybody has different use' [tr]`, `'not one person's work only' [tr]`, `Wife knows if he has eaten`
- Definition: the participant's own account of the arrangement: son for names and doctor, wife for the day and for knowing whether he has eaten, friends for sharing, a phone for when the wife is busy, and dependence at this age as ordinary and shared.
- Excludes: `Knowledge divided son, wife, self`, which is the division itself; ST3 is his summary and evaluation of it.
- RQ1.

### ST4. His own report of a dose checked by placement and by the wife
- Constituent codes: `Unsure whether already taken`, `Medicine placed in sight`, `Wife asks, he says yes wrongly`, `'yes yes' then forgets`, `Asks wife, then calls son`
- Definition: the participant doubts his own memory of having taken a dose, answers the wife's question affirmatively and is sometimes wrong, discovers misses by seeing the tablet still in its place, and under uncertainty asks the wife, then calls the son, taking nothing extra.
- Excludes: `Missed dose at morning meeting`, which is the condition of a lapse, not the check on it.
- RQ1.

### ST5. Lapses when the meal-anchored routine leaves the house
- Constituent codes: `Missed dose at morning meeting`, `Change and outside are hard`
- Definition: doses anchored to tea and lunch lose their cue outside, at meetings and functions, and change of regimen is the other named difficulty.
- Excludes: nothing retained; two-code sub-theme defended because the episode and its summary carry one mechanism together.
- RQ1.

### ST6. Alarms set by the son abandoned as unintelligible
- Constituent codes: `Alarm unintelligible among sounds`
- Definition: two alarms set by the son were not used because they were indistinguishable from the phone's other sounds.
- Excludes: nothing; single-code sub-theme defended because it is the account's only reported experience with a technology for medicine and its mechanism, configuration by another without explanation, is the baseline the hypothetical conditions in ST7 answer. Merging it into ST7 would mix a reported practice with hypotheticals; merging into ST4 would misfile a tool as a check.
- RQ1.

### ST7. Conditions on an agent: entered through the son, escalating after many misses, never watching
- Constituent codes: `Tell son after many misses`, `'not watching me all the time' [tr]`, `Trust via son's configuration`, `Son changes, app shows new`, `Simple to him, details to son`, `'Same like medicine' [tr]`, `Would close a demanding app`, `'not something to guess' [tr]`
- Definition: an agent would be trusted because the son configured it, would tell the son only after repeated misses and must not amount to being watched, would have its changes and stock alerts routed to the son, would speak simply to him and in detail to the son, would be more welcome if his group used it, must be correct, and would be closed if it demanded typing or answers. All hypothetical.
- Excludes: `Alarm unintelligible among sounds`, reported practice.
- RQ3, with RQ2 bearing through the escalation rule and the refusal of watching.

## Pass D: themes

### T1. Medication work as knowledge distributed by kind across son, wife, peers, and himself
- Organizing concept: the household and its surroundings hold different kinds of medication knowledge, each with its own holder, and the participant describes his part, recognition by sight, as one kind among several rather than as a deficit in the others.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Knowledge of the regimen divided by kind across son, wife, and self`, `The peer group as a source of experiential trust and indirect reminders`, `The network summarized as complementary uses`
- Definition and boundary: the division is volunteered at L51 and summarized at L219 and L247. The boundary is authority: the group can supply candidates and the wife can supply the day, but 'final decision doctor', and the son sits between the group and the doctor. Variation: the group is a source here in a way it was not for P01 to P03, who granted it talk only.
- Extracts: L51, L79, L107, L247.

### T2. Remembering secured by placement and the wife's question because his own report fails
- Organizing concept: the participant does not trust his own memory of having taken a dose, and the household's arrangement, tablets placed in sight and a wife who asks, exists to check that report rather than to replace his taking.
- Constituent sub-themes: `His own report of a dose checked by placement and by the wife`, `Lapses when the meal-anchored routine leaves the house`
- Definition and boundary: 'I say yes yes then sometimes I check and see no I did not take' is the mechanism. The boundary is that these are his accounts of the wife's checking; she is not interviewed, and whether she experiences the check as care or as labour is not in the corpus.
- Extracts: L23, L43, L31, L63.

### T3. An agent admitted through the son, escalating only after repeated misses and never watching
- Organizing concept: any tool enters this household warranted by the son's configuration and is acceptable on a two-sided rule, telling the son about repeated misses and never becoming continuous watching.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Alarms set by the son abandoned as unintelligible`, `Conditions on an agent: entered through the son, escalating after many misses, never watching`
- Definition and boundary: the abandoned alarms are the reported baseline (configured, unexplained, unused); the conditions are the hypothetical answer. The boundary is evidence grade: ST6 is practice and ST7 is described, and the theme joins them only as before and after of the same mechanism. The refusal of watching is the first explicit one in the phase and is volunteered inside an acceptance.
- Extracts: L35, L167, L179, L215.

Theme count: 3.
