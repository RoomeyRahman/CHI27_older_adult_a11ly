## What this account is about

A grandson who relies on the leaflet or label attached to the medicine to remind and give ten medicines: 'when I go and buy his medicine, I don't just leave it in the bag or box. There's usually a kind of leaflet or label attached that clearly says when to take which one. So I rely on that information to remind him and give him the right ones at the right time.' But if I'm not around or need a hand, I get help from other family members when needed.' 4. A pop-up 'prominent' enough: is the agent again competing for the caregiver's attention on a crowded phone?
