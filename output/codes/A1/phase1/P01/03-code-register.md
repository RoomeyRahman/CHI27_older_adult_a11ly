# P01, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 62. After reconciliation (merges of synonyms and near-duplicates sharing a mechanism, one split): 42. A first retained set of 34 was over target; a second reconciliation pass merged three more pairs that shared a mechanism and parked one thin single-line code, giving 30. Retained: 30. Parked: 12. Merged: 20. All 62 accounted for below.

Most parking was done by two criteria: instrument codes that answer only a direct design question with no unprompted appearance (the app-specification cluster at L147 to L231), and topic labels with no mechanism (inventory of doses, phone uses). Frequency was not used.

Hardest calls. (1) `Wants a timed reminder` is volunteered at the magic-wand question and recurs four times, all hypothetical. I retained it once, merged with its restatements, because it is the one unprompted design want in the account and it bounds what he will accept; parking it would erase his own stated wish in favour of my interest in the family. (2) `Forgetting under disruption` sits close to a deficit frame the framing commitments warn against. Retained, because his content locates the lapse in sociality and travel, not in a faculty, and that location is the finding; the code definition is written to hold that. (3) `Admits occasional missed dose` is elicited by a closed question and adds nothing beyond `Forgetting under disruption`; parked, absorbed.
