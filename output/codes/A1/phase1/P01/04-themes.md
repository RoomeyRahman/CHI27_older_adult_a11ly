- Constituent codes: `Memory as sole method`, `Unaided remembering as competence`, `Carrying fine, timing hard`, `Place is the information`, `Smartphone not for medicine`
- Definition: the participant keeps the regimen in memory and in a fixed place, and presents the absence of any external method, including an owned smartphone, as an achievement.

### ST2. Lapses located in social and spatial disruption rather than in memory

- Definition: missed doses are narrated as effects of travel, guests, and conversation on a fixed home routine, in the general case rather than as episodes, and recovered by a self-set rule.
- Excludes: `Son as decision partner`, which is the consultation that follows recovery and belongs with joint decision-making.
- RQ1.

### ST3. Disclosure to sons framed as a moral value

- Definition: telling the family about medicine is presented as the kind of person the participant is, privacy is equated with isolation, and family is placed beyond substitution.
- Excludes: `Checking received as help`, which is the reception of a specific practice, not the value that licenses it.
- RQ1.

### ST4. Decisions taken jointly with a son holding standing knowledge
- Constituent codes: `Son as decision partner`, `Sons know his medicines`, `Family as standing recourse`, `Community talk, family decides`, `No regular doctor`
- Definition: the unit that decides about a dose or a problem is the participant and a son together, on the basis of knowledge the sons already hold, with peers granted talk but not authority and no doctor in the regular loop.
- Excludes: `Elder son procures medicine`, which is a task role, not a decision role.
- RQ1.

### ST5. Household checking received as help
- Constituent codes: `Family asks if taken`, `Checking received as help`
- Definition: family members ask whether the dose was taken, contingently and without a fixed assignee, and the participant receives this as help rather than oversight.

### ST6. Roles distributed across specific network members

- Definition: procurement, interpretation, and device setup each sit with a named person, inside or outside the family, and the participant retains use while handing over setup.

### ST7. Conditions placed on a future reminder system

- Definition: a reminder is wanted, on condition that it is simple, escalates to a son only for a real miss, speaks respectfully, and leaves important decisions with the family. All hypothetical.
