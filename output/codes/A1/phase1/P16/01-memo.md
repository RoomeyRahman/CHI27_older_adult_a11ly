# P16, Phase 1: familiarization memo

Source: `Supplementary/Interviews/phase-1/Participants/P16.md`. Older adult, 76, woman, lives with her husband; thirteen medicines across three slots; keeps written notes, which her husband also writes; uses a phone but not for medicine. The shortest transcript in the phase with P15 (about 500 words), from the medicine-and-technology script, with one-line answers. No consent-exclusion record is filed. Deployment device is not recorded for this participant.

## What this account is about

A woman on thirteen medicines who manages them 'by myself' with a method: written notes. 'I have them noted down. Husband also notes for me, I take them according to that.' The husband reminds her 'whenever I forget' and would help manage if she could not. She does not usually miss doses; when she does it is because medicines run out or are unavailable. She uses a phone, not for medication, needs no technology, and her husband helps with any difficulty on it or with calling the doctor. She declines to suggest anything.

The account is the phase's clearest case of a written aid held jointly by a couple, and of a spouse as the sole network. It is also nearly bare of elaboration; almost every answer is a line.

## Analytic noticings, as questions

1. 'I have them noted down. Husband also notes for me.' Whose notes are they, and does joint authorship of the record make the aid the couple's rather than hers?
2. Thirteen medicines and no missed doses except on supply: is the written note doing for her what habit does for P05 and the prescription for P15?
3. The husband reminds, notes, manages if needed, helps with the phone, and helps call the doctor. Is the spouse the whole network, and is that a feature of the household or of the script?
4. 'No tools are needed' beside 'I have them noted down': are notes not counted as a tool, and what does that say about which aids read as aids?

## Absences

No children or other kin. No reading difficulty, no pharmacy, no episode. The script asks nothing about the household beyond who helps, so the spouse-only network may be the instrument's frame.

## Interactional conditions

Restatements at L29 and L37 confirmed briefly; `[elicited]`. The suggestion question at L69 is declined. Answers are short throughout; no probing.
