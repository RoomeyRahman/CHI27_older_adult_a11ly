# P06, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 76, the highest so far, from a long interview with heavy probing. After reconciliation (41 merges): 35. A second pass merged four codes that qualified a retained one and parked one thin self-report, giving 30. Retained: 30. Parked: 5.

Parking fell on inventory (household, nothing written), on an interactional artifact (the remark on interview length), and on one single-line self-report whose prompt is unknown. Frequency was not used; the group block is the longest in the transcript and became five codes on mechanism, not on length.

Hardest calls. (1) `'trying to remember better now' [tr]`: a volunteered self-improvement claim, but what prompts it (wife, son, interview) is not asked, and it adds no mechanism. Parked, and named in Pass E as the one closest to the line. (2) `Doubts the phone can know he leaves`: he reasons about the tool's limits himself, which cuts against an assumption of tool competence; merged into `'Outside there is no one' [tr]` so that the field reminder carries its own stated limit. (3) `Only my medicine, not others'`: a refusal of the agent as a channel for peer talk; merged into `'Family can handle the rest' [tr]` as part of the narrow remit he gives the agent, with the peer point kept in the definition.
