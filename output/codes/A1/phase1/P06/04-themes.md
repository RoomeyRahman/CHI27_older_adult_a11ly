# P06, Phase 1: Passes C, D, and E

## Pass C: sub-themes

Over the 30 retained codes only.

### ST1. Two patients under one roof reminding each other
- Constituent codes: `Spouses both on diabetes medicine`, `Spouses remind each other`, `Strips kept separate`, `Wife more careful, asks son`, `Wife knows if he ate`
- Definition: husband and wife share a condition and a regimen, remind each other reciprocally, keep their strips apart, and differ in disposition: she is more careful, asks the son, and knows whether he has eaten.
- Excludes: `Asks wife, calls son, no double`, which is his consultation under uncertainty rather than the reciprocal relation.
- RQ1.

### ST2. Reading, buying, and recording held by the son and a long-known pharmacy
- Constituent codes: `Son reads because more educated`, `New strip read by son or pharmacy`, `Son buys new prescriptions`, `Son writes or photographs, retells`, `Pharmacy knows us over years`, `Son's phone is the household's`
- Definition: everything that requires reading passes to the son, whose authority rests on education, or to a pharmacy that has known the household for years; the son's phone is the household's record and its channel to the world.
- Excludes: `Trust if son set it`, which is hypothetical and about the agent.
- RQ1.

### ST3. Recognition and habit as his own competence under the doctor's authority
- Constituent codes: `Knows the strip, not the name`, `Remembers himself after food`, `Asks wife, calls son, no double`, `Nothing taken without the doctor`
- Definition: he recognizes his tablet by the strip, remembers after food by habit, consults in order when unsure and never doubles, and takes nothing the doctor has not given.
- Excludes: `'Doctor tells what is for me' [tr]`, which is about the doctor against the group rather than about his own practice.
- RQ1.

### ST4. Time and reminding outside the house: the field and the gathering
- Constituent codes: `Farming has no fixed time`, `'Somebody always reminds somebody' [tr]`
- Definition: field work and harvest move the dose because they do not follow the clock, and at gatherings the shared problem produces undirected mutual reminding.
- Excludes: `Wife knows if he ate`, the home side of the same division, placed with the marriage.
- RQ1.

### ST5. The farmers' group as experiential authority under the doctor
- Constituent codes: `Farmers' talk includes medicine`, `Five fine outweigh one bad story`, `'not trying to sell me anything' [tr]`, `'Doctor tells what is for me' [tr]`, `'Same like new seed' [tr]`
- Definition: farmers who share work and meet daily supply testimony weighed by number and trusted for its lack of motive; the doctor decides what is for him; adoption of a tool would follow the group as a new seed does.
- Excludes: `'Somebody always reminds somebody' [tr]`, the group as reminder rather than as authority.
- RQ1, with RQ3 bearing through the seed analogy.

### ST6. Conditions on an agent: for the field, controlled by family, trusted through the son
- Constituent codes: `Son's demo abandoned after wrong press`, `Family controls, he only takes`, `'Outside there is no one' [tr]`, `Son can call 'baba take medicine'`, `Missed press not a problem`, `Recognition checked by family`, `Trust if son set it`, `'Family can handle the rest' [tr]`
- Definition: a prior demo was abandoned; an agent would be useful where the wife is not, controlled by son or wife, trusted because the son set it, escalating as the son's call, with recognition checked by family, a missed press not counted against him, and everything beyond the reminder left to the family. One reported code, the rest hypothetical.
- Excludes: `Son's phone is the household's`, reported practice about the present.
- RQ3, with RQ2 bearing through control, escalation, and remit.

Sub-theme count: 6.

## Pass D: themes

### T1. Medication work divided between a spouse who shares the regimen and a son who reads
- Organizing concept: in this household the everyday work is carried by two patients reminding each other, and the literate work is carried by a son and a pharmacy, with the participant's own part being recognition, habit, and deference to the doctor.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Two patients under one roof reminding each other`, `Reading, buying, and recording held by the son and a long-known pharmacy`, `Recognition and habit as his own competence under the doctor's authority`
- Definition and boundary: the division runs on literacy and on co-presence. The boundary is that the son's wife and grandchildren, four of the six in the house, do no medication work in the account; and that the wife's disposition is reported by him.
- Extracts: L47, L123, L159, L43.

### T2. Outside the house, field time governs lapses and the farmers' group governs trust
- Organizing concept: what happens to medicine outside the home is set by two collective conditions, the field's refusal of clock time and the group's testimony, and both are described as shared rather than personal.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Time and reminding outside the house: the field and the gathering`, `The farmers' group as experiential authority under the doctor`
- Definition and boundary: 'Farming doesn't follow clock' and 'We all have this problem' locate lapse and remedy in the collective. The boundary is the doctor, who decides what is for him, and the wife, who gates his group talk. Variation: the group is a stronger source here than for P01 to P03, closer to P04.
- Extracts: L71, L215, L111, L207.

### T3. An agent for where the wife is not, controlled by the family and trusted through the son
- Organizing concept: the agent's place is the field, where no one reminds; its control belongs to son or wife; its trust travels through the son as a medicine's does through the group; and its remit stops at the reminder.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Conditions on an agent: for the field, controlled by family, trusted through the son`
- Definition and boundary: single sub-theme theme, kept apart for evidence grade. 'Outside there is no one' is the theme's centre. Boundary: he doubts the phone can know when he leaves, so the field reminder carries its own stated limit; and the whole is premised on a phone he does not own.
- Extracts: L267, L263, L199, L311.

Theme count: 3.
