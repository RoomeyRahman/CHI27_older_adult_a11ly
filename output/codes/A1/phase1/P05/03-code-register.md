# P05, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 60. After reconciliation (27 merges): 33. Retained: 30. Parked: 3.

Parking fell on instrument codes (sound, picture, small information) and on inventory (household, regimen size). Frequency was not used. The peer-group block was merged down to two codes because the interviewer's probing, not the participant's emphasis, produced its length.

Hardest calls. (1) `Irritated, then reads it as help` against `Children worry and check`: both are about the daughter's checking, but one is its reception and the other her explanation of it; kept separate, because the master synthesis needs to see whether irritation and the generational reading travel together across accounts. (2) `Says yes, then realises no` could be merged into `Reminded, not handed`; kept separate, because the first names the mechanism by which asking helps and the second names the boundary of what the family does. (3) `'Otherwise family is easier' [tr]` is a closing line and could be parked as summary; retained for high load, since it is the only statement in the phase so far that compares family and technology as competing means.
