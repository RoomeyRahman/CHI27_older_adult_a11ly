# C01, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 94, the longest transcript in the phase. After reconciliation (56 merges): 38. A second pass merged eight more pairs sharing a mechanism, giving 30. Retained: 30. Parked: 3.

Parking fell on inventory (counts, dose times, phone numbers). Frequency was not used; the design block, which is more than half the transcript, became nine codes on mechanism.

Hardest calls. (1) `'no proper way to know' [tr]` and `'I don't know if they took it or not' [tr]` name one mechanism, her knowledge failing in absence; merged under the in-vivo name that states it as a limit. (2) `'remember the medicines for us' [tr]` is a strong in-vivo line about the agent as the household's memory; merged into `'should not feel like a big medical thing' [tr]` because both concern the agent's register and place, with the plural kept in the definition. (3) `'they say they can manage themselves' [tr]` and `'I don't want to control everything' [tr]` are the parents' contestation and her response; kept separate, because the affiliation codebook needs the contestation and the yielding as distinct moves.
