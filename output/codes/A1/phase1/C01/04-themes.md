### ST6. Conditions on an agent: names the medicine, waits, tells her, never checks secretly

- RQ3, with RQ2 bearing through visibility of checking and of changes.

## Pass D: themes

### T1. Caregiving as a check that works when present and becomes trust when absent
- Organizing concept: the caregiver's work is a check on the dose that depends on her presence, and when she is away the check degrades to telling beforehand, calling, and finally trusting, because there is no way to know.
- Constituent sub-themes: `A check that works by presence and fails as knowledge in absence`, `The mother's lapses read as time lost to occupation, not misunderstanding`
- Definition and boundary: 'If I cannot then I just have to trust that she will take it. There is no proper way to know' is the theme. The boundary is that the mother's lapses are the caregiver's account of them; the mother is not in this transcript, and whether she is one of the older adults in the corpus is not filed.
- Extracts: L13, L109, L269, L89.

### T2. Tools that add tasks and authorities that stay put
- Organizing concept: the household's attempts at aids have failed by adding a task, and its authorities, the pharmacist for reading and the doctor for deciding, are fixed points any tool must leave in place.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Aids and records that became tasks or never began`, `Reading and deciding kept with pharmacist and doctor`
- Definition and boundary: 'You have to remember to put the medicine in the box' and 'Doctor is still doctor' are the two halves. Boundary: the pillbox summary was the interviewer's; the mechanism is hers.
- Extracts: L65, L121, L141, L401.

### T3. A role that arrived without decision and is held without wanting to control
- Organizing concept: the caregiver's position was never assigned or announced, is mildly contested by the parents when they feel confident, and is held by her as help rather than control, which she defends as care even when a system would do the calling.
- Constituent sub-themes: `A role that just happened, held without wanting to control`
- Definition and boundary: single sub-theme theme, kept apart because these four codes are the phase's only direct answers to the specified questions on how the role was settled and contested, and they are the human baseline for the affiliation practices. Boundary: all four are answers to direct questions; the content exceeds the questions.
- Extracts: L357, L349, L353, L373.

### T4. An agent she would configure that names the medicine, waits before telling her, and never checks secretly
- Organizing concept: the agent this caregiver describes takes over her absent check without taking over her position: she configures it, the parents are told what she changes, it tells her only a real miss after a delay, and it never checks without the parents knowing.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Conditions on an agent: names the medicine, waits, tells her, never checks secretly`
- Definition and boundary: single sub-theme theme for evidence grade. 'I don't think it should secretly check. They should know' and 'they should know if I change something' are the visibility norms; 'Outside there is no one' is the agent's place. Boundary: hypothetical throughout.
- Extracts: L325, L289, L229, L301.

Theme count: 4.
