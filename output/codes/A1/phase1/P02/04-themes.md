# P02, Phase 1: Passes C, D, and E

## Pass C: sub-themes

Over the 30 retained codes only.

### ST1. A light regimen held by habit and place
- Constituent codes: `Fixed place as method`, `Habit time, not clock time`, `One medicine makes recall easy`
- Definition: one tablet a day is kept in one place and taken at a felt time, and the participant attributes the ease of this to the regimen's simplicity rather than to any method or faculty.
- Excludes: `Takes it myself`, which is about who performs the act, not how it is remembered.
- RQ1.

### ST2. Reminding accepted while administering is refused
- Constituent codes: `Takes it myself`, `Refuses to be given medicine`, `Help and self-doing together`
- Definition: the participant accepts being reminded and refuses being handed the medicine, holding help and self-doing together as compatible.
- Excludes: `Setup by family, use unchanged`, which applies the same separation to a device and belongs with network roles.
- RQ1; bears on RQ3 through what kind of help a tool may offer.

### ST3. Lapses located in disruption and departure
- Constituent codes: `Forgetting under disruption`, `Forgets to carry when travelling`, `No specific episode recalled`, `Morning peer exercise group`
- Definition: missed doses are narrated as effects of guests, outings, the peer group running long, and the attentional load of leaving the house, in the general case; the community setting is the same one that asks after the sick.
- Excludes: `Family asks when he forgets`, which is the response to a lapse rather than its condition.
- RQ1.

### ST4. Unassigned mutual reminding within the household
- Constituent codes: `Family asks when he forgets`, `'We help each other' [tr]`, `Reminder received as family help`, `Pre-departure family check`, `Family already knows regimen`
- Definition: whoever is present asks, cued by seeing him busy or leaving, on the basis of knowledge the whole family already holds, and he receives this as help justified by kinship alone.
- Excludes: `Younger members handle phones`, which is an assigned competence, not a diffuse one.
- RQ1.

### ST5. Medicine information bounded at the family's edge
- Constituent codes: `Medicine details family-only`, `One trusted member may change`
- Definition: the household may know and the community may not, and within the household knowing is distinguished from the authority to change, which he would give to one trusted person.
- Excludes: nothing retained; two-code sub-theme defended because the boundary is volunteered twice and the change-authority code is the account's only statement on settings.
- RQ1 for the boundary; RQ2 for the change authority (hypothetical).

### ST6. Named roles beyond the household
- Constituent codes: `Pharmacy interprets prescription`, `'Everybody has their own role' [tr]`, `Would still verify with people`, `Younger members handle phones`, `Setup by family, use unchanged`
- Definition: prescription reading sits with the pharmacy, medical problems with the doctor, daily things with the family, device work with the young; a tool's output would still be checked with one of these people.
- Excludes: `Agent should refer upward`, which is a condition on the agent, not a description of the network.
- RQ1.

### ST7. Conditions on a reminder agent
- Constituent codes: `Wants a timed reminder`, `Reminder must be automatic`, `Many steps defeat him`, `Delay before family alerted`, `App as backstop for busy family`, `Over-notification breeds inattention`, `Agent should refer upward`, `'Don't make us feel' [tr]`
- Definition: a spoken, automatic reminder that asks one action, waits before worrying the family, covers the family's own lapses, does not talk constantly, knows when to send him to the doctor, and does not make older people feel they understand nothing. All hypothetical.
- Excludes: `Setup by family, use unchanged`, placed in ST6 because it describes who does what rather than what the agent must do.
- RQ3, with RQ2 bearing through the delay and backstop codes.

Sub-theme count: 7.

## Pass D: themes

### T1. Taking kept as one's own act while remembering is the only help wanted
- Organizing concept: the participant partitions medication work into remembering, which he will accept help with from anyone, and taking, which he keeps as his own act, and he locates lapses in disruption so that the partition costs him nothing.
- Constituent sub-themes: `A light regimen held by habit and place`, `Reminding accepted while administering is refused`, `Lapses located in disruption and departure`
- Definition and boundary: the refusal at L141 is the hinge. He has already said twice that he takes it himself; when offered someone to give it to him, he refuses and names what he does need: 'I just need to remember'. The boundary is that the regimen is one tablet, which he himself cites as why it is easy, so the partition may be easier to hold here than in a heavier regimen.
- Extracts: L141, L45, L57, L177.

### T2. Reminding diffused across whoever is present, with medicine information bounded at the family
- Organizing concept: this household has no caregiver role; reminding is mutual and cued by sight, roles are named only beyond the household, and the line between who may know and who may not falls at the family's edge.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Unassigned mutual reminding within the household`, `Medicine information bounded at the family's edge`, `Named roles beyond the household`
- Definition and boundary: 'we help each other' and 'whoever is around' describe reminding as a property of the household rather than a job. Outside it, roles are tidy (pharmacy, doctor, the young with phones). The boundary case is the community: it asks after the sick and helps with errands, and it is exactly the group that must not hear the reminder. Variation from P01: no son is named as decision partner; the network is flatter.
- Extracts: L381, L169, L417, L437.

### T3. A reminder agent accepted as a backstop for a busy family, on conditions of restraint
- Organizing concept: the agent is admissible if it covers the family's lapses as well as his, asks almost nothing of him, waits before alarming anyone, and keeps to small things.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Conditions on a reminder agent`
- Definition and boundary: single sub-theme theme, kept apart because every code is hypothetical. The backstop reading (L481) is the theme's centre: he does not accept the agent as a replacement for family or for himself, but as cover for when 'family members are also busy'. Boundary: the delay before escalation is framed as sparing the family worry, not as protecting his standing.
- Extracts: L481, L473, L249, L521.

Theme count: 3.
