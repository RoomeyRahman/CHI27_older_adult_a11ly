# P02, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 83, a high count driven by a long, heavily probed interview in which many turns are confirmations that nonetheless carry a small addition. After reconciliation (39 merges, no splits): 44. A first retained set of 34 was over target; a second pass merged three codes into the setting or practice they qualify and parked one thin recovery code, giving 30. Retained: 30. Parked: 11. Merged: 42.

Most parking was done by two criteria: instrument codes answering an offered feature with no unprompted appearance (modality, language, text size, option count, departure reminder), and topic labels with no mechanism (inventory of phones, the walkthrough). Frequency was not used; `Reminder must be automatic` appears once and is retained for its load, while `Handwriting very difficult` is elicited and parked despite recurring.

Hardest calls. (1) `App as backstop for busy family` is a single line inside a hypothetical, yet it inverts the interviewer's premise and is the account's one unprompted statement about how the agent relates to the family. Retained. (2) `Delay before family alerted` and `Family access if for helping` are both elicited and hypothetical; I retained the first because it states a condition (time before escalation) and parked the second as a bare yes with a purpose clause that `Medicine details family-only` already carries. (3) `Takes it myself` and `Refuses to be given medicine` share a mechanism, and I nearly merged them. Kept separate: one is a standing claim volunteered before any offer, the other a refusal of a specific offered arrangement, and the refusal is the sharper evidence that reminding and administering are different things to him.
