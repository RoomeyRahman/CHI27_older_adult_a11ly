## What this account is about

A young son who 'sees a lot of it because we stay together' and does not manage every dose: 'Sometimes I remind them. Sometimes they remember themselves. Sometimes I check.' The household's method is habit, the name written on the back of the medicine leaf, and whoever is at home: 'Somebody sees. Somebody reminds. Somebody checks. It is like that.' The account turns on an episode: his father forgot a morning dose, his sugar dropped, and he was taken to hospital. 'We were busy with other things... Nobody checked at that exact time.' The episode changed the household: 'before that we just assumed he will remember. Now I feel maybe we should not depend only on memory... We keep checking.' And it produced pressure: 'If nobody checks then I worry.'

His closing generalizes the mechanism: 'If one person forgets somebody else usually remembers. But sometimes everyone is busy. That is when the problem happens.' For an agent he wants voice for his parents and a notification for himself only when they do not confirm, 'Because of what happened with my father', and he holds that his parents would accept it: 'It is family. We already ask each other. So app reminder is just another way.' He reads prescriptions better than his parents but sometimes needs the pharmacy; he tells his parents not to change medicines on others' advice while admitting the group influences him.

## Analytic noticings, as questions

1. 'We just assumed he will remember.' Is assumption the household's default before a consequence, and does checking begin only after harm?
2. 'Nobody checked at that exact time.' Is the lapse narrated as a gap in the household's coverage rather than his father's memory?
3. 'If nobody checks then I worry.' Is his pressure the caregiver's version of the older adults' 'somebody should keep an eye'?
4. 'App reminder is just another way.' Does the agent enter as a new form of an existing family act, and what does that do to its standing?
5. 'Who it is for': two parents, two regimens. Is the agent asked to hold the household's plurality rather than one patient's schedule?
6. He tells his parents not to follow others' advice and admits he follows the group himself. Is the son a gate on advice for the parents that he does not apply to himself?
