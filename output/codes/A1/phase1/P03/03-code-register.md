# P03, Phase 1: Pass B code register

## Trim rationale

Candidates after Pass A: 59. After reconciliation (24 merges, no splits beyond those made in the recode pass): 35. Retained: 30. Parked: 5.

The count came in high on retention because this account is dense with named network roles, and each role is a distinct mechanism the master synthesis will need to see separately (who knows, who keeps, who reminds, who relays, who buys, who remembers the consultation). Parking was done on instrument codes (modality preferences, colour, the doctor number) and on inventory. Frequency was not used.

Hardest calls. (1) Whether to keep `Daughter's anger at the lapse` separate from `Feels better when checked`. Both are the reception of checking; the first is scolding, the second comfort. Kept separate, because merging them would settle by assumption what the two extracts leave open, whether scolding is received as the same care as quiet checking. (2) `'I just take what they give' [tr]` and `'I just follow' [tr]` are one position in two phrasings, one volunteered and one elicited; merged, with the volunteered extract as anchor. (3) `Own report may be wrong` is a single line and volunteered; retained for high load, since it is the only place in the phase so far where an older adult asks for oversight on the grounds of her own unreliability as a witness.
