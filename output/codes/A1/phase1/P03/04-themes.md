# P03, Phase 1: Passes C, D, and E

## Pass C: sub-themes

Over the 30 retained codes only.

### ST1. The daughter as holder of knowledge, record, and stock
- Constituent codes: `Daughter knows the names`, `Daughter keeps and arranges medicines`, `Daughter holds the record`, `Stock is daughter's to track`, `Daughter remembers the consultation`, `Daughter visits daily`
- Definition: a non-resident daughter who comes daily holds the regimen's names, the strips and their sorting, the prescription and its photograph, the written list, the stock count, and the memory of what the doctor said.
- Excludes: `Husband asks, daughter tells`, which is reminding rather than holding; `Daughter should enter changes`, which is hypothetical.
- RQ1.

### ST2. Deference to the daughter accounted for as prudence
- Constituent codes: `'Why I will take chance' [tr]`, `'I just take what they give' [tr]`, `Calls daughter for the decision`, `Relatives' advice routed to daughter`, `Women's talk, family decides`
- Definition: the participant places herself as the terminal act in a chain from doctor through daughter, justifies this as not taking chances with something that matters, and routes all outside advice through the daughter.
- Excludes: `Feels better when checked`, which is about reception of checking, not about decision.
- RQ1.

### ST3. Recognition by sight as the participant's own competence
- Constituent codes: `Recognition by sight defended`, `Big letters only, with glasses`, `Pharmacy reads for us`, `Changes confuse, family retells`
- Definition: she identifies her tablets by strip, colour, and packet and can pick hers from three, cannot read small text or names, has the pharmacy read for the household, and is thrown by a changed strip until the family has told her repeatedly.
- Excludes: `Wants time, picture, voice`, which is a design want in the same medium but hypothetical.
- RQ1.

### ST4. Reminding by whoever is present, spouses reciprocally
- Constituent codes: `Husband asks, daughter tells`, `Spouses remind each other`, `Missed dose, sister visiting`, `Forgetting with guests or out`, `Gathering shifts meal cues`
- Definition: the co-resident husband asks and sometimes hands the tablet, the two spouses remind each other, the daughter is called when the husband also forgets, and lapses come when company or a changed meal time removes the cue.
- Excludes: `Daughter's anger at the lapse`, which is the affective reception of a check rather than the check.
- RQ1.

### ST5. Checking received as comfort, including its scolding form
- Constituent codes: `Daughter's anger at the lapse`, `Feels better when checked`, `Own report may be wrong`
- Definition: being checked is a comfort she prefers to managing alone, the daughter's anger and repetition are reported as part of that checking without complaint, and she wants oversight because her own report of having taken a dose may be wrong.
- Excludes: `Wants daughter told of non-taking`, the mechanism she proposes, which is hypothetical and sits with the record.
- RQ1; bears on RQ2 through the grounds on which oversight is wanted.

### ST6. Tools not installed or not explained remain unused
- Constituent codes: `Abandoned alarm not understood`, `'Nobody has put one' [tr]`
- Definition: an alarm set by the daughter and never explained was ignored, and no app is used because nobody has installed one; non-use is explained by others' acts before her own knowledge.
- Excludes: nothing retained; two-code sub-theme defended because the two codes share a mechanism (installation and explanation as another's act) that no other sub-theme carries.
- RQ1.

### ST7. A shared record the daughter configures and the family reads
- Constituent codes: `Wants daughter told of non-taking`, `Missed press resolved by asking`, `Daughter should enter changes`, `Simple for her, details for daughter`, `Wants time, picture, voice`
- Definition: she wants the daughter told when she has not taken, the daughter to enter medicines and changes, the family to see everything, the detail to go to the daughter and the simple instruction to her, and a missed confirmation to be resolved by the daughter asking rather than by the record.
- Excludes: `Own report may be wrong`, the reason, placed with reception of checking.
- RQ3, with RQ2 bearing through who configures and who is told.

Sub-theme count: 7.

## Pass D: themes

### T1. Medication work held by the daughter and accounted for as prudent deference
- Organizing concept: the daughter holds nearly every part of the work except the swallowing, and the participant presents this not as incapacity but as the prudent way to handle something that matters.
- Constituent sub-themes: `The daughter as holder of knowledge, record, and stock`, `Deference to the daughter accounted for as prudence`
- Definition and boundary: the two sub-themes are the arrangement and its moral account. The boundary is the husband, who 'knows some' and reminds but does not hold the record; and the participant's own reserve, that she can manage if nobody is there. Variation from P01 and P02: here the delegation is nearly total and named to one person.
- Extracts: L43, L51, L119, L147.

### T2. Everyday competence and reminding organized around sight and presence
- Organizing concept: what the participant does herself runs on recognition by sight, and what others do for her runs on who is physically present, so that the daily practice is sensory and proximate while the knowledge is elsewhere.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Recognition by sight as the participant's own competence`, `Reminding by whoever is present, spouses reciprocally`
- Definition and boundary: she picks the strip; the husband asks; a changed strip or a changed meal time breaks both. The boundary is that these are her own claims about her competence and her husband's role; the husband is not interviewed.
- Extracts: L71, L123, L35, L195.

### T3. Checking wanted as comfort, including when it scolds, because her own account may fail
- Organizing concept: the participant wants to be checked, receives the daughter's anger as part of that checking, and grounds the wish in her own unreliability as a witness to her dose.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Checking received as comfort, including its scolding form`
- Definition and boundary: single sub-theme theme, kept apart because it is the account's answer to the care-or-oversight question and that answer has two edges, comfort and scolding, which the extracts hold together and I did not want to resolve by placing it under T1. Boundary: the scolding is reported once, about carrying medicine when visiting, and its reception is inferred from the absence of complaint, not from a statement.
- Extracts: L127, L211, L107.

### T4. Configuration and correction of a shared record left to the daughter and to asking
- Organizing concept: any tool enters this household through the daughter, who installs, explains, enters, and changes it, and its record is corrected by a person asking rather than by the record itself.
- Constituent sub-themes: `Tools not installed or not explained remain unused`, `A shared record the daughter configures and the family reads`
- Definition and boundary: the abandoned alarm is the baseline case (installed, not explained, unused) that the imagined configuration answers (installed, explained, differentiated). The boundary is evidence grade: ST6 is reported practice, ST7 is hypothetical, and the theme holds them together only as a before and after of the same mechanism.
- Extracts: L27, L95, L163, L199.

Theme count: 4.

Counter-readings. T1: the alternative is that 'I just take what they give' is resignation, and the prudence talk is a face-saving gloss on dependence. The extracts do not settle it, but the register of the account is matter of fact throughout and she defends her own competence precisely elsewhere (L71), which a resigned account would not bother to do. The reading holds, and the matrix note records the alternative. T2: the alternative is that sight recognition is a limitation the account is dressing as competence. It loses on L71, where she volunteers a test she would pass. T3: the alternative is that she does not receive the scolding as care but has no standing to complain about it to a researcher. I cannot exclude this; the reception is inferred from absence of complaint. I concede the inference is weaker than the comfort statement at L211, and the theme's definition says so. T4: the alternative is that the record's subordination to asking is an artifact of the interviewer's question about forgetting to press. The question supplied the scenario; her answer supplied the remedy (the daughter asks), and the remedy is what is coded.

Two parked or merged codes closest to the line: `Phone given by daughter, limited use` (parked) and `Others' doses cue her own` (merged). The first stays parked; provenance is noted in the configuration codes and the phone's uses do no work. The second stays merged; a peer cue is acknowledged and immediately ranked below family, so it belongs with the women's-talk boundary.

Change forced by Pass E: none structural. This account is richer than P01 or P02 and I believe the four-theme structure is warranted by it, but the scolding reading (T3) is the one I hold least firmly.
