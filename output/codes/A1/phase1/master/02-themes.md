# Phase 1 master synthesis: Passes C and D (sub-themes, themes, development log)

Grouping is over the 40 master codes in `01-code-synthesis.md` only. Each master code lists its participants there; sub-themes and themes below name codes by id and exact name. OA and CG markers follow the code cell in `03-matrix.md`.

## Pass C: sub-themes (12)

### S1. The act kept, the knowing shared
- Constituent codes: M01 `Taking kept as one's own act`, M02 `Unaided remembering claimed as competence`, M03 `Recognition by strip, packet, and place`
- Definition: older adults keep the swallowing as their own act and defend either unaided memory or recognition by sight as their competence, while conceding that names, reading, and bringing are others'; caregivers describe the same partition from their side.
- Excludes: M09, the distribution of what others hold.
- RQ1. Carried by 21 units, both sides.

### S2. Lapses of occupation and supply, and the unreliable self-report
- Constituent codes: M04 `Lapse located in occupation and disruption`, M05 `Own report of a dose unreliable; a question restores it`, M06 `'yes yes' that does not secure the act`, M07 `Defer or skip, never double; consult before recovering`, M08 `Supply, not memory, as the failure point`
- Definition: doses are lost to guests, travel, work, and talk, or to a shop that ran out; the older adult's own report of having taken a dose is unreliable and a kin's question restores it; recovery defers or skips and never doubles, usually after asking.
- Excludes: M25, the household's collective failure, placed with the caregiver's problem.
- RQ1. 21 units, both sides.

### S3. Knowledge, record, and the doctor channel held by an adult child
- Constituent codes: M09 `Regimen knowledge divided by kind across kin`, M10 `Prescription and record in an adult child's custody`, M11 `An adult child as the channel to the doctor`, M12 `Procurement and stock held by kin`
- Definition: names, the paper, the consultation, and the buying sit with a son, daughter, daughter-in-law, or husband, divided by literacy and presence; three men keep the doctor channel themselves.
- Excludes: M13 to M16, the sources outside the household.
- RQ1. 24 units, both sides.

### S4. Pharmacist, doctor, and peers as sources ranked by authority
- Constituent codes: M13 `The pharmacist as a reader known over years`, M14 `The doctor as the gate on any change`, M15 `Peer talk granted talk, never authority`, M16 `Peer experience as a ground of trust`
- Definition: the pharmacist reads and remembers, the doctor alone changes, peers supply talk and experience that builds confidence but never decides; the same ranking is given by both sides.
- Excludes: M17, the peer as reminder.
- RQ1. 15 units, both sides.

### S5. Reminding by presence and its reception
- Constituent codes: M17 `A peer's dose as an indirect reminder`, M18 `Reminding as an unassigned household property`, M19 `Reminding divided by presence: co-resident asks, distant calls`, M20 `Checking received as help, safety, or comfort`, M21 `Checking received with irritation, scolding, or reproach`, M22 `Told about a miss, not watched all the time`
- Definition: who reminds is a matter of who is present, from a peer mentioning their tablet to a spouse asking to a daughter calling; the older adults receive it as help and safety, with irritation and scolding at the edges, and they accept being told about a miss while refusing to be watched.
- Excludes: M23, the account of delegation.
- RQ1; the human baseline for RQ2. 19 units, both sides.

### S6. Delegation as prudence and relief, with small refusals
- Constituent codes: M23 `Delegation accounted for as prudence and relief`, M27 `Small refusals inside a large delegation`
- Definition: handing knowledge and decision to a child is justified as not taking chances and as relief from thinking; inside it sit a few refusals, a changed packet, a skipped medicine, a regimen negotiated down.
- Excludes: M26, the caregiver's account of the role.
- RQ1. 9 units, older adults.

### S7. The caregiver's knowledge problem and the household's shared failure
- Constituent codes: M24 `The caregiver's problem as knowledge in absence`, M25 `The household's attention failing all at once`
- Definition: caregivers cannot know whether a dose was taken when away and want to be told; the household's coverage fails when everyone is busy at once, twice with medical consequence.
- Excludes: M19, the check when present.
- RQ1; bears on RQ2. 8 units, mostly caregivers.

### S8. A role that just happened
- Constituent codes: M26 `A caregiver role that just happened, held without wanting to control`
- Definition: the caregiver's position began from need, was never decided or announced, is yielded when the parents are confident, and is held as help rather than control.
- Excludes: nothing; single-code sub-theme defended because it is the phase's only direct evidence on how a role is settled, and it is the ceremony baseline.
- RQ1; the baseline for RQ2 and RQ3's ceremony dimension. 4 units.

### S9. Aids: abandoned, standing, and delegated
- Constituent codes: M28 `Aids set by others and left unused`, M29 `A standing alarm or written note that reminds but does not compel`, M30 `Setup and change delegated to the young, use retained`, M31 `Trust in a tool transferred through the person who set it`
- Definition: aids set by others are abandoned; the few standing alarms remind without compelling; configuration is the young's and use the older adult's; a tool is trusted through the person who set it.
- Excludes: M32 to M39, which describe a tool not yet present.
- RQ1 and RQ3. 17 units, both sides.

### S10. Conditions on an agent: place, record, voice, escalation
- Constituent codes: M32 `The agent's place is where the family is not`, M33 `The record subordinate to the act`, M34 `The reminder must speak and name the medicine`, M35 `Bounded repetition, then one kin told after a delay`
- Definition: an agent is admitted where the family is not, its record yields to the act and to a human question, it speaks the medicine's name, and it escalates to one kin after bounded repeats. All hypothetical.
- Excludes: nothing retained.
- RQ3; bears on RQ2. 17 units, both sides.

### S11. Conditions on an agent: visibility, address, bounds, simplicity
- Constituent codes: M36 `Checking and changes visible to the older adult`, M37 `Simple to the older adult, details to the kin, respectfully`, M38 `The agent bounded below the doctor and to information`, M39 `Simplicity as the condition of use`
- Definition: an agent must not check secretly, must speak simply and respectfully to the older adult and in detail to the kin, must not decide medicine, and must be simple or it will be dropped. All hypothetical.
- Excludes: nothing retained.
- RQ3. 17 units, both sides.

### S12. Technology declined or displaced
- Constituent codes: M40 `Technology declined or displaced onto others`
- Definition: aids not needed yet, needed by others, or not one's place to suggest; the design role declined by the self-managers and by one rural woman.
- Excludes: nothing; single-code sub-theme defended because refusal is a position the other sub-themes cannot hold without smoothing it.
- RQ3. 9 units, both sides.

## Pass D: themes (4)

### MT1. Medication work partitioned so that taking stays the older adult's act while knowing, reading, and deciding are distributed across kin, pharmacist, and doctor
- Organizing concept: the household's medication work is divided by kind, and the division protects the older adult's standing: the act and the recognition are theirs, and what they cannot read or decide is held by named others whose authority is ranked.
- Constituent sub-themes: S1, S3, S4
- Participants: all 24 units. Two-source rule: cleared many times over. RQ1. Contribution: C1 (the human affiliation baseline: assignment).
- Draws on per-unit themes: P01 T2, P02 T1 and T2, P03 T1 and T2, P04 T1, P05 T1, P06 T1, P07 T1 and T2, P08 T1, P09 T1, P11 T1, P13 T2, P15 T1, P17 T1, C01 T2, C02 T2, C03 T2, C04 T1, C07 T1.
- Boundary and variation: the three men who manage alone (P11, P15, P17) and the couple who share notes (P16, Phase 1) are the variation, and they show the division is assigned, not given by age; the caregiver accounts add that the caregiver's own literacy has a limit the pharmacist covers.

### MT2. Lapses located in the household's attention and in supply rather than in memory, with the household's question as the check on an unreliable self-report
- Organizing concept: what fails is a distribution of attention across occupied people, or the shop, and what repairs it is a question from whoever is present, which works because the older adult's own report is unreliable and the question resolves either way.
- Constituent sub-themes: S2, S5, S7
- Participants: 22 units (15 older adults, 7 caregivers). Two-source rule: cleared. RQ1. Contribution: C1, and the empirical ground for C3's claim that checking is care as well as oversight.
- Draws on per-unit themes: P01 T1, P02 T1 and T2, P03 T2 and T3, P04 T2, P05 T2, P06 T2, P07 T2, P08 T2, P09 T2, P10 T1, P12 T1, P13 T1, C01 T1, C02 T1, C03 T1, C04 T1 and T2, C05 T1, C08 T1.
- Boundary and variation: the reception of checking runs from comfort and safety (P07, Phase 1) through irritation reframed as help (P05, Phase 1) to scolding (P03, Phase 1) and reproach (P08, Phase 1); and the two households in which everyone was busy at once (C02, C03) mark where the arrangement fails.

### MT3. Delegation and checking accounted for as prudence, relief, and care, in roles that arrived without decision and are contested only in small refusals
- Organizing concept: both sides give a moral account of the arrangement: older adults as prudence and relief, caregivers as help rather than control; the role was never decided aloud, and what contestation exists is small and inside the delegation.
- Constituent sub-themes: S6, S8, plus M20, M21, M22 from S5 as the reception codes it draws on
- Participants: 16 units (13 older adults, 3 caregivers). Two-source rule: cleared; M36 within S11 is the only `[single-instance]` code in the phase and it sits in MT4. RQ1 as the baseline for RQ2 and RQ3. Contribution: C1 (gifting, contestation, revocation, and the absence of ceremony among humans) and the seeded tensions of delegated dependence as agency and oversight as intimacy.
- Draws on per-unit themes: P03 T1 and T3, P05 T2, P07 T3, P08 T3, P09 T1, C01 T3, C02 T3, C07 T2.
- Boundary and variation: older adults supply the affirmative reception and caregivers the account of the role; the two sides do not describe the same households, since pairing is unfiled, and the widow in her son's house (P07, Phase 1) is the most affirmative and the least free to be otherwise.

### MT4. Aids and agents admitted only where the family is not, configured by the young, subordinate to the doctor, and made to speak the medicine's name to a hearer who may not read
- Organizing concept: every aid the households have tried failed by adding a task or by being set without explanation; the agent they describe is admitted into the arrangement's gaps, configured by a child, bounded below the doctor, speaking the medicine's name, escalating to one kin after a delay, and never checking secretly.
- Constituent sub-themes: S9, S10, S11, S12
- Participants: all 24 units. Two-source rule: cleared for every code but M36, which carries `[single-instance]`. RQ3, elicited; bears on RQ2 through direction and visibility. Contribution: C3 (the design conditions in the participants' words, before the build) and the formative ground for the tool role.
- Draws on per-unit themes: P01 T3, P02 T3, P03 T4, P04 T3, P05 T3, P06 T3, P07 T4, P08 T3, P09 T2, P10 T2, P11 T2, P12 T3, P13 T3, P15 T2, P16 T2, P17 T2, C01 T4, C02 T3, C03 T3, C04 T3, C05 T2, C06 T1, C07 T2, C08 T2.
- Boundary and variation: everything in S10 and S11 is hypothetical; S9 and S12 are reported. The refusals in S12 and the standing alarms in S9 are the variation, and the caregiver who wants a paid tool addressed to himself (C07, Phase 1) sits at the far end from the woman who declines to suggest anything (P09, Phase 1).
