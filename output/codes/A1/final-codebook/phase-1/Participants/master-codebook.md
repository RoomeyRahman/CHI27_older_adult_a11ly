# Phase 1, Participants (older adults): master codebook

Master codebook for the 16 Phase 1 older-adult transcripts (P01 to P17, no P14), re-analysed from the full transcripts. Four themes in one line: medicine as family work; where the routine breaks and nothing they can use catches it; what the phone should do for them; and where the agent belongs in the family. Themes 3 and 4 are the prototype's requirements in the older adults' own terms. Codes are in plain words; every quote is verbatim, attributed by participant id, phase, and line. Theme definitions, sub-theme meanings, and the link from each theme to the next are in `themes.md`.
