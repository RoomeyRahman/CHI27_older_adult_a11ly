# Phase 1: final codebook (older adults and caregivers)

Final Phase 1 codebook, built by reading the older-adult and caregiver master codebooks together (`Participants/master-codebook.md`, `Caregiver/master-codebook.md`). Four themes carry one story: medication as distributed family work; where that work breaks at a distance; why existing aids do not fit; and what both sides expect of an agent placed in the family. Codes stay in participants' plain words and draw on both roles; every quote is verbatim, attributed by id, phase, and line. Theme definitions, sub-theme meanings, and the link from each theme to the next are in `themes.md`; the narrative is in `analysis/plans/result/story_phase_1.md`.
