# Phase 2: final codebook (older adults and caregivers)

Final Phase 2 codebook, built by reading the older-adult and caregiver master codebooks together. Four themes carry the deployment story: inherited allegiance; relocated reminding with retained care; person over record and doctor over agent; relational outcomes and borrowed trust. Every definition ends with its evidence grade: lived (done or seen during the two-week deployment), elicited (a position under an offered frame), hypothetical (an answer to a conditional), believed-capability (a behaviour attributed to the agent that the build record excludes; no use claim). Codes in plain words; quotes verbatim, attributed by id, phase, and line. No household pairing is filed. The narrative is in `analysis/plans/result/story_phase_2.md`.
