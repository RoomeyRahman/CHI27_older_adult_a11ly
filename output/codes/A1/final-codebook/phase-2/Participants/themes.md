# Phase 2, Participants (older adults): themes

Each theme has a definition, a boundary or counter-case, the RQ and contribution it serves, and a consequence that leads to the next theme. Each sub-theme has a paragraph on what it means. Evidence grades (lived, elicited, hypothetical, believed-capability) are in the code definitions of the codebook in this folder.

## 1. Inheriting the Agent into Family Practice

**Definition.** Older adults heard the agent say whom it worked for, granted its request to involve family, and let it become shared through the kin who checked it. They account for this as trust and as another way of a help the family already gave, with the older adult first and the family second.

### The Announcement as Legibility and Slight Exposure

The spoken statement of whom the agent served was strange, then clarifying; it made the family's behaviour legible as well as the agent's. Said in front of others it exposed a little, and the older adult wants to be told before the family is.

### Granting the Request as an Act of Trust Toward Kin

Six older adults narrate the request-and-grant prompt: an unanswered reminder, a request, agreement, a kin's question. The grant is accounted for as trust because nobody hands the tablet, and as another way of a help already given.

### Shared Through the Kin Who Checked It

The agent became the household's when a daughter, son, or daughter-in-law checked it or heard it, not through configuration.

### A Plural Principal With an Order and a Consent Condition

Everyone answered both, the older adult first; one participant named her own agreement as the condition for telling family.

**Boundary or counter-case.** Grants were lived by six older adults; consent as a condition was named by one (P14). Self-managers (theme 2) granted nothing.

**RQ and contribution.** RQ2 assignment and gifting; C1, C2 (direction, ceremony)

**Consequence.** Because the agent's allegiance was inherited from the household's own division, what changed was not who decides but who reminds first, the next theme.

## 2. Relocated Reminding, Retained Care

**Definition.** The first reminder moved from spouse or child to the agent and kin asked less, but they still ask and that asking is care, sometimes irritating, that the agent cannot carry. The phone does not know if the older adult has eaten; the family does. Self-managers refused a family project.

### The First Reminder Moved to the Agent

The phone or the ringing now reminds first; kin ask less for normal doses and ask again when the agent tells them something.

### Family Asking as Care the Agent Cannot Replace

Irritation at repeated asking and knowing it is care sit in one sentence; a son's question is a different act from a phone message.

### Situated Knowledge Kept With the Family

The phone knows only what is put into it; the spouse knows whether the older adult has eaten or is outside.

### Counter-Case: Not a Family Project

Self-managing older adults accept the agent as a layer and decline family notification and any collectivisation of their routine.

**Boundary or counter-case.** Counter-case: P11, P15, P17 keep reminders to themselves; P17 had no family routine to replace.

**RQ and contribution.** RQ2 contestation inside care; C1

**Consequence.** Because the family's asking remained the check that mattered, the agent's record had to yield to the person, the next theme.

## 3. Person Over Record, Doctor Over Agent

**Definition.** Older adults read their own silence as busyness or a forgotten press and insist a person check before the record is believed; a false alarm to kin is the failure they fear. They used the decline to spare kin worry, want the first chance and then one kin told only of a real miss, keep the doctor and the prescription above the agent, and found the agent stale after a change until someone updated it. Described pushback was accepted only as reasoning about a design that was not built.

### Silence as Busyness and the Record Below the Act

No answer means busy, deferred, unheard, or taken and not pressed; the agent must not assume a miss and a kin's question settles it.

### The Decline Used to Spare Worry

Older adults stopped the family alert when they had taken the dose, so kin would not worry or think they missed.

### Described Pushback Accepted in Principle (Believed-Capability)

Irritation at being asked again after a no, then acceptance because medicine is dangerous; reasoning about a described mechanism, not use.

### First Chance, One Kin, Real Miss

Escalation that waits, repeats, then tells one person chosen by knowledge or presence, only for a real miss, silence, or empty stock.

### The Doctor Decides and a Wrong Reminder Can Be Stopped

The prescription or notes are the authority; the agent reminds what was decided and can be stopped when wrong.

### Knowledge Only as Current as Its Updater

After a change the agent reminded of the old schedule until updated; a change must be told to everyone.

**Boundary or counter-case.** M07 is believed-capability throughout; the weakening veto was not built. Two lived stale-schedule episodes (P10, P11).

**RQ and contribution.** RQ2 revocation; RQ3 tool role, revocability, visibility

**Consequence.** With the agent bounded to reminding and its record held below the person, its outcomes are relational and small, the last theme.

## 4. Relational Outcomes of a Two-Week Deployment

**Definition.** The streak became a small thing kin talked about, its breaks made small by family and read as something to check rather than lose. Trust was earned by testing the agent against routine, alarm, notes, or prescription and borrowed from the kin who set it. The reminder acted as a second memory rather than a replacement, wanted as a voice with the name, with stock alerts and no extra work.

### The Streak as a Family Object

Family noticed and praised the streak; a break felt bad and was made small by kin; more read a changed count as something to check than as a loss; sharing for encouragement, not pressure, and never outside the family.

### Trust by Testing and Through Kin

Reminders compared with routine, alarm, notes, or prescription before depending on them; trust through the child who set it up.

### A Second Memory, Not a Replacement

The reminder reinforced timing and anticipation; people still know and some still forget; dependence on the phone alone is refused.

### Voice, Stock, and Nothing That Takes Over

A voice with the name suits non-readers; a warning before medicine runs out; the agent helps without taking over.

**Boundary or counter-case.** Shared family scores were not built; probationary mode was not built; the testing was participant-initiated. P13 still forgets sometimes.

**RQ and contribution.** RQ3 outcomes and mechanisms; C3

**Consequence.** These outcomes close the older adults' account and hand the design its terms for the next iteration.
