# Phase 2, Participants (older adults): master codebook

Master codebook for the 17 Phase 2 older-adult transcripts (P01 to P17). Four themes: how the agent was inherited into family practice; what moved and what stayed; how the person was held above the record and the doctor above the agent; and the relational outcomes. Every definition ends with its evidence grade: lived (done or seen during the two-week deployment), elicited (a position under an offered frame), hypothetical (an answer to a conditional), believed-capability (a behaviour attributed to the agent that the build record excludes; no use claim). Codes in plain words; quotes verbatim, attributed by id, phase, and line. No household pairing is filed.
