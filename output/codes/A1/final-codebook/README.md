# final-codebook

Final codebooks in plain words, derived from the audit trail in `../phase1/`, `../phase2/`, and `../cross-phase/`. Read `theme.md` first for the story across both phases (current practice and tension, barriers, expectations, what the deployment showed, contribution).

Each codebook has five columns: Code, Definition, Sub-theme, Theme, Example quote. Each folder has a `themes.md` giving every theme with a definition, its sub-themes with what they mean, a boundary or counter-case, the RQ and contribution served, and the consequence that leads to the next theme. Theme and sub-theme names follow CHI convention (analytic noun phrases); codes stay in participants' plain words.

- `phase-1/Participants/`: master-codebook.md, themes.md (16 older adults, RQ1).
- `phase-1/Caregiver/`: master-codebook.md, themes.md (8 caregivers, RQ1).
- `phase-1/`: final-codebook.md, themes.md (both roles merged, 40 codes, 4 themes).
- `phase-2/Participants/`: master-codebook.md, themes.md (17 older adults, RQ2 and RQ3; grade at end of each definition).
- `phase-2/Caregiver/`: master-codebook.md, themes.md (8 caregivers).
- `phase-2/`: final-codebook.md, themes.md (both roles merged, 40 codes, 4 themes).
- `theme.md`: earlier cross-phase overview (superseded for Phase 1 and Phase 2 by the per-phase `themes.md` files and by `analysis/plans/result/story_phase_1.md` and `story_phase_2.md`).
- `tsv/`: every codebook and themes file as tab-separated values, same columns.

Quotes verbatim (checked by `quote_check.py`), attributed by participant id, phase, and line. No real name; no dash. Phase 2 grades: lived, elicited, hypothetical, believed-capability (attributed to the agent but not built; no use claim). Household pairing unfiled; no row pairs a caregiver with an older adult. Generated 2026-09-06.
