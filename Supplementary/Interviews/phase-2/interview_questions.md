**Appendix B: Post-Deployment Evaluation Interview Protocol**

_Note for Researchers: This protocol is explicitly structured around the proposal’s conceptual framework: the agent’s shifting roles (Tool, Coach, Advocate), the Affiliation Ledger, and the Bangladeshi context of collective care. It seeks to capture the ethnography of how the agent’s allegiance is negotiated in the household._

**Section 1: The Affiliation Ledger & Negotiating Allegiance**

- **Main Goal:** Understand how the family navigates the agent's explicit declarations of loyalty and the "ceremony" of changing roles.
- The agent was designed to announce in plain Bangla exactly who it was serving at any given time (e.g., working just for you, or working for your family). How did it feel to hear the agent openly declare its loyalty?
- _[Probe: Did this make the technology feel more transparent, or did it feel intrusive in a household setting?]_

- There is a specific "request and grant" process for changing the agent's role from private (just you) to shared (alerting family). Walk me through the last time the agent’s role shifted in your home.
- _[Probe: Did handing over the agent's loyalty to your children feel like you were giving up your independence, or did it feel like a mutual act of trust?]_

**Section 2: Veto Power, Risk, and Silence**

- **Main Goal:** Evaluate the design mechanisms of the weakening veto and the interpretation of silence.
- When the agent wanted to alert your family about a missed dose, you had the power to veto or stop it. Did you ever use this veto?
- _[Probe: The system is designed so that if health risks grow, your ability to veto weakens audibly rather than silently. How did you react to the agent pushing back on your veto?]_

- Sometimes, people do not respond to the agent at all. When you were silent, how did the agent handle it?
- _[Probe: Did you feel the agent understood your silence as a normal part of your routine, or did it wrongly treat your silence as a failure?]_

**Section 3: Care Collectivism & "Checking as Love"**

- **Main Goal:** Assess how the agent integrates into the Bangladeshi model of care, where medication is a shared practice rather than a private burden.
- Before this agent, checking if you took your medicine was often a way for your family to show affection. Now that the agent does the checking and alerting, how has that changed your interactions with your family?
- _[Probe: Do you miss the manual check-ins from your children, or does the agent’s notification give your family a new reason to talk and show care?]_

- Think about a time when a prescription was unclear or you had to make a sudden health decision. Did the agent act as a helpful part of your family’s "kitchen table" discussion, or did it get in the way?

**Section 4: Gamification as a Relational Trigger**

- **Main Goal:** Evaluate points and streaks not as individual behavioral nudges, but as occasions for the family to express care.
- When you reached a high score or a long streak, how did your family react to it?
- _[Probe: Did your daughter or son ever use your score as a reason to praise or encourage you?]_

- Think back to a time when a streak was broken. In a shared family setting, did losing that streak cause anxiety, or did it lead to mutual encouragement from your family?
- _[Probe: Did you prefer keeping your score private, or did sharing the score with your family make the experience better?]_

**Section 5: Probationary Trust & Retraining (The Empirical Arc)**

- **Main Goal:** Capture the unanticipated findings from early deployments—probationary testing and retraining the internal body clock.
- When we first introduced this agent, did you put it on a "probationary period"?
- _[Probe: How did you test the agent to see if it was trustworthy before you actually started relying on it for your health?]_

- Over the past few weeks, do you feel that relying on the agent has replaced your own memory, or has it actually retrained your internal sense of when a dose is due?

**Section 6: Closing & The Plural Principal**

- **Main Goal:** Final reflections on the agent serving a multi-stakeholder household.
- If you had to summarize: who do you feel this AI agent ultimately works for? You, your family, or both?
- _[Probe: Under what specific conditions is it acceptable for the agent to stop acting as your private tool and start acting as your family’s advocate?]_
