## Related Work drafting note, 2026-09-06

Five frameworks carry load in `/output/Related-Work.md`: principal and agent framing ([62], [79], [38], [32]), intermediated use ([98], [33], [1]), articulation and invisible work ([106], [85], [8]), interdependence and relational autonomy ([13], [30], [72]), and postcolonial computing ([49], [26]). Four were already committed rows; the postcolonial row was committed but unused until now and carries the paragraph on systems deployed in the Global South, without which that paragraph reads as a catalogue of regional failures.

Excluded from Related Work after applying the Section 10 deletion test: self-determination theory ([95], [116], [113], [14]), Goffman ([34]), logic of care ([80]) and ethic of care ([112]), and asset-based community development ([60]). In each case deleting the citation would not change the conclusion of any Related Work paragraph. [80] additionally remains **open** pending a test against the data, and committing it in Related Work would pre-empt that test.

## Enforcement note

The rule in CLAUDE.md Section 10 applies at every `/polish` pass: if a paragraph's theoretical citation could be deleted without changing the paragraph's conclusion, either rewrite the paragraph so the theory does work or delete the citation. Three rows above are the likeliest failures of that test, because they are easy to cite decoratively: [95], [10], and [61]. Each is marked *supporting* for that reason.
