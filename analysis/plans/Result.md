# Results Writing Plan: From a Fragile Dose to Movable Allegiance

## Governing Story

`analysis/plans/result/story.md` is the narrative source. The Results section follows one medication event outward: routine disruption removes familiar cues; family checking becomes necessary and contested; existing tools cannot complete the handoff; the agent carries timing and exposes family involvement; its records create new uncertainty; families revise its role; and those revisions form the care framework.

The section does not begin by declaring that care already works. It begins with the condition that motivates the research: otherwise established care becomes fragile when time, place, people, supply, and devices stop aligning.

## Subsection Chain

### 4.1 Care Became Fragile When Routines Moved

**Dramatic question:** Who notices a dose when the ordinary cues and people are no longer present?

**Opening evidence:** P01's home-versus-travel account, C03's caregiver pressure when nobody checks, and P05's irritation at repeated checking.

**Analytic broadening:** Move from the disrupted dose to the A2 themes `Routine transitions expose weak coordination and uncertain verification` and `Medication work is distributed through differentiated household roles`. Explain taking, recognizing, interpreting, supplying, reminding, checking, and authorizing. Bring in paper, packages, notes, alarms, button phones, personal smartphones, shared smartphones, and proxy operation.

**Collectivist boundary:** Use P10's “we don't live in isolation,” but retain P17's self-management and refusal. Bangladesh remains the empirical scope; the Global South is not treated as a uniform finding.

**Exit:** The care gap lies in a fragile handoff, which creates the next question: why did existing tools fail to carry it?

### 4.2 Existing Tools Signaled Time but Could Not Complete the Handoff

**Inherited problem:** A caregiver needs evidence, an older adult needs room to act, and a disrupted routine has separated both from the usual cues.

**A2 themes:** `Existing aids fit tasks but miss household handoffs` and `Families expect technology to support bounded care network roles`.

**Paragraph movement:** First show variable tool fit: notes and alarms sometimes suffice; alarms may lack medicine meaning; pillboxes may add refilling; small text and multi-step operation may move work to kin. Then convert each limit into an elicited expectation: semantic Bangla reminders, few actions, visible checking, time before escalation, bounded access, proxy setup with inspection, and continuing clinical authority.

**Bridge table:** Map five Phase 1 tensions to formative expectations, what the deployed agent actually did, and what remained unbuilt. This table is the connective tissue between phases.

**Terminology:** Use `the agent`, not `the prototype`. State deployed behavior precisely and list prescription capture, stock sensing, selected-recipient routing, patterned silence, and risk grading as unbuilt.

**Exit:** The agent enters at the handoff. Phase 2 evaluates whether it can carry timing without taking over care.

### 4.3 The Agent Carried Time, but Families Still Carried Care

**Inherited problem:** The agent now reminds and records, but the care network must decide where its work begins and ends.

**A2 themes:** `The agent supports routines while people retain care` and `Older adults control when the agent involves family`.

**Paragraph movement:** Return to mobility through P06, then report felt timing with the leading-prompt qualification. Move into device participation through P05's proxy-setup account, shared-device operation, Bangla speech, large text, few controls, and requested images or selectable modes. Next establish direction through C05's caregiver-first account and P10's spoken-allegiance account. End with caregiver reports of reduced clock watching but retained responsibility.

**Reported improvement:** Portable cues, direct action after delegated setup, clearer direction, and attention focused on exceptions. No significant, adherence, clinical, or objective workload claim.

**Exit:** The agent has shifted timing and attention, but its confirmation record cannot establish ingestion. That new record becomes the problem carried into Section 4.4.

### 4.4 The Agent Made Care Visible, but Not Certain

**Inherited problem:** An unconfirmed record can involve family without determining whether medicine was taken.

**A2 themes:** `Caregivers act on uncertain exceptions, not automatic judgments`; `Streaks show care work, while medicine remains primary`; and `Friends and community support care, while doctors guide medicine`.

**Paragraph movement:** Begin with P05's taken-but-unconfirmed episode, then show symmetrical frustration for older adults and caregivers. Move directly into verification and the three-state expectation. From confirmation uncertainty, transition to the streak as another representation built from incomplete records. Use streak grief and family repair to bound the coach role. Finally widen visibility to reciprocal peer care, confusion, clinical authority, and chosen sharing.

**Gamification connection:** The streak is not a feature detour. It inherits the gap between recorded confirmation and medication action, making some care visible while excluding unrecorded work.

**Exit:** Because no representation resolves context, ingestion, or authority, the final section organizes how families keep the agent's position revisable.

### 4.5 A Care Framework for Moving the Agent's Allegiance

**Inherited problem:** More reminders, records, or sharing cannot settle who should act next.

**Synthesis:** Tool, coach, and advocate are revisable positions, not a progression toward autonomy. Direction, visibility, revocability, and ceremony describe how each position becomes accountable. The Affiliation Ledger names the deployed announcement and request-and-grant sequence, with undeployed mechanisms kept at the future edge.

**Framework cycle:** Locate existing work; identify disruption; assign a bounded role; make allegiance perceptible; verify and revise. Map each stage to an empirical basis and residual expectation.

**Ending:** Return to the opening dose. The routine moves, the agent reminds, the record remains unresolved, and the care network decides whether allegiance should move. The empirical contribution is the repeated negotiation, while Discussion develops its theoretical and design claims.

## Theory Alignment

Articulation work organizes the fragile handoff and the redistribution of timing, checking, recording, and repair. Interdependence organizes delegated setup and reciprocal care without making family involvement compulsory. Circumspection organizes verification as competent participation. Relational gamification remains supporting, with the streak interpreted as an incomplete record of care rather than a behavioral incentive.

No theory citation enters Results. The ledger records where these frameworks carry analytic load.
